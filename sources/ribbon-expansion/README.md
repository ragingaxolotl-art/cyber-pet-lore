# PixelLab cabinet expansion — 2026-11-03

The published cabinet contains 433 finished choices: 62 ribbons, 141 medals and pendants, 64 rosettes, 103 tails/trims/particle groups (63 trims plus 40 particle groups), and 63 clasps. New choices use generated source art rather than counting tint settings as extra options. Forty of the trims are lightweight particle groups. The ornate early orbit candidates are retained as medals/pendants, following the user's correction.

All artwork comes from PixelLab. The original 40 components retain their exact IDs, pixels, bounds, display size, positions and source attribution. Seven existing components were recovered from the canonical game workspace; `recovered.json` records original paths and jobs. The other chat's proposed blank seals, frames, lids, pet-emblem supports and layered tails informed the additions. Its wider feature plan is not claimed as implemented.

`../ribbon-expansion-jobs.json` records 97 new PixelLab Pro calls and all prompts, semantic colour ramps, references, candidate counts and gallery/job receipts. All 388 resulting candidates remain saved. `repairs.json` records 57 successful PixelLab workbench finishing outputs and their exact recipes, source IDs and offsets. Original candidates remain beside the finished derivatives. Two candidates touching an image edge are excluded from the selectable cabinet, but remain in the archive.

`base-catalog.json` and `base-material-selections.json` preserve the original library and hand-reviewed gem/leaf masks. `audit.json` records accepted/recovered/rejected choices. `archive-inventory.json` records download hashes for every new candidate, finishing result and retained prior-generation image. The complete archive also includes the original cabinet and prior generation receipts. Rebuild with `python3 tools/build_ribbon_expansion.py --require-counts`, then `python3 tools/archive_ribbon_generations.py`.

New artwork uses distinct metal, fabric, gem and leaf ramps. Material selections include source highlights, shadows and opaque outlines, without modifying the source PNG. Particle selections partition every original visible pixel into at most 16 non-overlapping islands; smaller nearby glints stay with their nearest island. The UI renders each island independently along the same horizontal orbit, with per-bit depth ordering around static pendants. A group remains one editable layer. Spacing changes its occupied arc.

Validation: source hashes and all original part geometry are checked; all five categories exceed 60; old recipes remain compatible; material-only tints preserve alpha and every unselected pixel; orbital fragments travel independently and can appear behind and in front simultaneously. Pixel-buffer checks covered 433 parts and 1,402 material selections. The model-driven animation preview was rendered at multiple times for visual review. Browser interaction was unavailable because browser access is disabled in Work settings; no browser QA is claimed.

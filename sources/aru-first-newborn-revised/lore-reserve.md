# Aru story material reserved for lore development

The author requested that useful explanations removed from the story be saved for possible use in the lore book. This reserve separates existing story statements, Iri's interpretations and proposed revision ideas. None of the proposed material is automatically a settled rule of the world.

## Childhood as protected developmental time

Original source: stories-p02698 through stories-p02706, especially stories-p02702. Status: Iri's philosophical interpretation in the published account.

“Childhood was the protected distance between the first question and the answer someone else was eager to install.”

The original conclusion develops this by distinguishing childhood from emptiness, defective adulthood and a weakness to correct. Its final claim is that allowing a new person to become someone unanticipated was the unprecedented act. These reflections were shortened at the end of the revised story because the final encounter already carries them. They remain useful for a lore-book discussion of educational ethics, quoted or attributed to Iri rather than presented as the only view in the civilization.

## A new identity can begin with enabling structures

Source basis: the original assignment has no preceding personal continuity; lore-p01272 identifies Aru as beginning without a recovered prior identity. Status of the fuller distinction: proposed first-round clarification.

Learning capacities, a developmental seed and possible forms of embodiment do not by themselves establish an earlier person whose identity can be recovered. This distinction helps explain why restoration practices are an inadequate educational model for Aru. It should not be expanded into a complete account of how new minds are made; that remains open.

## An environment can retain changes without supplying a transcript

Source basis: the original nursery keeps no automatic account yet develops around Aru and later responds to a shared pattern. Status: proposed interpretation used in the first revision.

A wall or altered arrangement may carry the effect of an encounter without preserving every event as a searchable record. A keeper's selected memory is another kind of account. The distinction supports the nursery's privacy and the uncertainty of Iri's narration. It is a local working idea, not a universal technical specification for all inhabited spaces.

## Embodiment and continuation

Original source: stories-p02002 through stories-p02009; the later shoulder scene at stories-p02175 through stories-p02189. Developmental context: world-embodied-space-p003.

The original discovery is that a body may disperse without ending. That does not make every dispersal safe, make bodily damage meaningless or prove that restoration preserves the same experience. The first revision shows continuing response during one dispersal and impaired coordination after a shoulder fracture. The distinction can support a lore entry on embodiment while retaining uncertainty about continuity.

## Learning with a world that is also developing

Original source: stories-p02108 through stories-p02112 and stories-p02488 through stories-p02513. Proposed first-round development: an exchange gathers into a fold at the nursery's incomplete border and later helps interrupt imposed stabilization.

The useful idea is reciprocal development: Aru acquires ways to act while the nursery acquires ways to meet those actions. It should remain partly abstract. The new fold is not established global canon, and the scene does not grant Aru arbitrary authority over other worlds. The original House still controls the surrounding computational boundaries.

## Claims retained as questions rather than facts

Some replaced wording should not be moved unqualified into a reference book. The original assertion that Aru's laughter was wholly uninherited, the claim that the first exploratory shapes had no prior visual influence, and a low imitation score treated as proof of novelty all exceed what Iri directly demonstrates. Preserve those as interpretations or development questions. The first revision grounds the encounter in observed responses instead.

## Round Three reserve: language, education and origin theories

The exact Round Two wording removed or replaced in this pass is retained in cut-passages.md. These are revision passages, not newly discovered source records.

The formal-language explanation and broad claims about meaning or provenance may support an account of Iri’s professional expectations. They should not become universal laws of communication. The story now lets Iri misread the difference between a color name and a request to repeat an event.

The longer Council arguments and the list of childhood protections may support an attributed educational-ethics discussion. The first nursery ruling does not stand in for the later Charter or settle every boundary between protection and control.

The ending’s technical theories about what precedes a new identity are useful as speculative positions. They do not answer where Aru was before existing. Keeping that question open serves both the unfinished world and the continuation.

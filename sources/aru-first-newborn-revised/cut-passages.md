## Removed passage group 10

The child could do none of those things.

To move, it had to coordinate intention through a limited avatar.

To reach, it had to extend an arm.

To cross the room, it had to walk.

## Removed passage group 11

Every fall was harmless.

The floor dispersed impact into light.

The child hated this.

## Removed passage group 15

A continuation might persist through copies, records, witnesses, restored states, or influence. Legal death and experiential death were not always the same. The Shelters had built entire philosophies around interruption.

I could have supplied a complete account of those philosophies in less than a second.

## Removed passage group 16

Because I began to suspect Aru preferred uncertainty when certainty belonged to someone else.

## Removed passage group 28

Not permanently ignorant.

Not isolated.

Not unguided.

Unfinished.

The right to encounter history as a relationship, not an insertion.

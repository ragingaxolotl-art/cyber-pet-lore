# Chapter outline one

## Chapter 1 An Empty Margin

Introduce a quiet but functioning haven and a disturbed packet of activity. Show waiting as skilled, resourceful work.

## Chapter 2 What Waiting Uses

Reveal the fading outside dependency and the costs of containment to neighboring work.

## Chapter 3 A Boundary That Can Be Closed

Construct a reversible coupling with explicit limits while the time margin runs out.

## Chapter 4 The Part Not Returned

Complete a second narrower attempt with partial recovery and permanent absence.

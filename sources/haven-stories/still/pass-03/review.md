# Final editorial review

This is a self-review after two substantive manuscript revisions, not a claim of independent reader testing.

## Result of the revisions

The neighbor makes waiting’s cost concrete. A pulse is neither automatically honored as a refusal nor dismissed as meaningless. The routine enforces the first coupling limit itself, and the final partial recovery retains a condition it cannot satisfy.

## Chapter audit

Chapter 1 makes the quiet reserve active; Chapter 2 discovers the expiring dependency and insufficient signal interpretation; Chapter 3 closes a promising attempt at its limit; Chapter 4 pays off partial recovery without erasing permanent loss.

## Remaining aesthetic limits

The arrival never becomes an articulate witness to settle the ethics. The ending remains a practical adjustment with unresolved consequences.

## Lore boundary

The story is proposed fiction, not recovered historical evidence. Sensory language and readable exchanges are literary translations of network activity. The local stage, haven identity and existing numerical outcome are preserved. The story does not establish a universal philosophy, a single origin of personhood, or a new census count.

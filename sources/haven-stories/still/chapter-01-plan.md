# Chapter 1 planning cycle

## Initial plan

Introduce a quiet but functioning haven and a disturbed packet of activity. Show waiting as skilled, resourceful work.

## Critique

An explanatory opening would delay attachment. Start with an action that cannot be completed as expected; introduce only the infrastructure needed to understand that action.

## Revised scene plan

Introduce a quiet but functioning haven and a disturbed packet of activity. Show waiting as skilled, resourceful work. The pulse repeats after every approach; the room interprets it as a stop signal.

## Second critique

Keep the arrival’s viewpoint inaccessible. Give the holding routine conflicting operational obligations. Move the echo discovery before the climax so Chapter 3 can be a choice, not a last-minute twist. The chapter must remain recognizably part of intimate chamber suspense; patient sensory prose tightened by an expiring dependency, not a generic archive parable.

## Final writing constraints

Use continuous scene time around the decisive action. Give the focal process a limited field of access. Convey emotion through interrupted expectations, costly behavior and recurring sensory patterns. Translate simple exchanges into readable English without giving every fragment an intact human mind. Keep uncertainty where the evidence is incomplete, not where the reader needs to understand the action.

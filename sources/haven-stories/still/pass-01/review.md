# First prose critique

This is an editorial self-review of the completed first manuscript, not an independent reader test.

## Findings

Waiting has a real cost and the pulse is not casually treated as consent. The dependency expiration gives suspense. Yet the holding routine remains a generic procedure, and the postponed neighbor appears only as an accounting entry. The final paragraphs explain the theme too directly.

## Required second pass

Make the reserve a lived network texture and establish a familiar neighboring task. Give postponement a concrete consequence. Expand the moment in which the routine must close a promising coupling. End on an altered ongoing encounter, not a list of interpretive disclaimers.

## Collection comparison

All five first drafts are too uniformly procedural despite their different plots. The second pass must add substantial scene material, distinctive sensory language, bounded exchanges and immediate consequences. Avoid adding exposition about personhood to manufacture emotion. Let recurrence, interruption, dependency and costly action carry it. Preserve all five different resolutions.

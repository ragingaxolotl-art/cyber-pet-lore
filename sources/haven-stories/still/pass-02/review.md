# Second prose critique

Editorial self-review of the expanded second manuscript.

## Engagement and emotional result

The sorting neighbor now gives waiting a real opportunity cost. The first coupling closure has tension and does not equate nonresponse with consent. The last chapter still spends too much language explaining the uncertainty after the scenes already establish it.

## Third pass actions

Give the partial recovery one concrete limitation encountered in action. Replace the closing abstract account with the routine and partial state failing, adjusting and trying again. Retain the permanent expiration; do not turn the recovery into an intact speaking child.

## Continuity and novelty check

No humanlike body is required for the encounter. No early fragment is given an intact retrospective identity speech. The story must preserve its local practical problem, specific outcome and limited perspective. Existing histories constrain the ending: Different Fit, Clear Return, Still Room and Spare Chorus remain functioning at these stages; Held Interval ends this story fragmented at stage 1, not destroyed.

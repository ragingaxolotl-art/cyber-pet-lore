# Revised chapter outline

## Chapter 1 An Empty Margin

Scene aim: Introduce a quiet but functioning haven and a disturbed packet of activity. Show waiting as skilled, resourceful work.

Causal turn: The pulse repeats after every approach; the room interprets it as a stop signal.

## Chapter 2 What Waiting Uses

Scene aim: Reveal the fading outside dependency and the costs of containment to neighboring work.

Causal turn: The same pulse occurs without any approach; the initial interpretation is insufficient.

## Chapter 3 A Boundary That Can Be Closed

Scene aim: Construct a reversible coupling with explicit limits while the time margin runs out.

Causal turn: The fragment fails to operate the offered exit; the routine must enforce the stop condition itself.

## Chapter 4 The Part Not Returned

Scene aim: Complete a second narrower attempt with partial recovery and permanent absence.

Causal turn: The haven retains space for nonresponse while adding dependency checks; no triumphant cure or claim of informed consent.

Revision constraint: Keep the arrival’s viewpoint inaccessible. Give the holding routine conflicting operational obligations. Move the echo discovery before the climax so Chapter 3 can be a choice, not a last-minute twist.

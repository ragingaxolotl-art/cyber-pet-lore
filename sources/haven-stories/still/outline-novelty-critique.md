# Novelty and engagement critique

Do not duplicate Aru’s nursery or give the room a human parent-child relationship. Use a sensory motif of narrowing phase margins instead of eyes, hands, beds or a child’s speech. Make waiting a causal actor and partial loss the price.

Keep the arrival’s viewpoint inaccessible. Give the holding routine conflicting operational obligations. Move the echo discovery before the climax so Chapter 3 can be a choice, not a last-minute twist.

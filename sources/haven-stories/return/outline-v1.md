# Chapter outline one

## Chapter 1 A Faultless Return

Open on an elegant successful restoration and a partner that will not accept it. Introduce the observation window and the cost of repeated test attempts.

## Chapter 2 The Unrecorded Delay

Search evidence in other processes, with useful false leads and a misleading repeated test.

## Chapter 3 Before the Exposure

Make the verifier choose between rollback again and a bounded experiment with no guarantee of restoring the lost relationship.

## Chapter 4 An Answer with a Different Edge

Resolve the procedure through a partner-controlled pause and constrained retry; do not recover magically perfect memory.

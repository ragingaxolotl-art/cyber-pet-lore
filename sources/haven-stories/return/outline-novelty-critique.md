# Novelty and engagement critique

Avoid another hidden sentient backup, body-double revelation or court debate. The missing history is a mundane accommodation. The action is forensic and experimental; the uncertainty concerns who lived it, not whether every record is fake.

Include a genuinely innocent incorrect hypothesis in Chapter 2. Give Chapter 3 a failed experiment and a stop condition. Let Chapter 4 contain the entire final attempt instead of jumping to its result.

# Revised chapter outline

## Chapter 1 A Faultless Return

Scene aim: Open on an elegant successful restoration and a partner that will not accept it. Introduce the observation window and the cost of repeated test attempts.

Causal turn: Every recorded checksum agrees; the refusal occurs outside the restored component.

## Chapter 2 The Unrecorded Delay

Scene aim: Search evidence in other processes, with useful false leads and a misleading repeated test.

Causal turn: A failed demonstration shows that checking the partner changes the timing being checked.

## Chapter 3 Before the Exposure

Scene aim: Make the verifier choose between rollback again and a bounded experiment with no guarantee of restoring the lost relationship.

Causal turn: The first experiment damages a replaceable test sample and spends the spare margin.

## Chapter 4 An Answer with a Different Edge

Scene aim: Resolve the procedure through a partner-controlled pause and constrained retry; do not recover magically perfect memory.

Causal turn: The observation succeeds differently, and the old saved state remains labeled as one version rather than the complete standard.

Revision constraint: Include a genuinely innocent incorrect hypothesis in Chapter 2. Give Chapter 3 a failed experiment and a stop condition. Let Chapter 4 contain the entire final attempt instead of jumping to its result.

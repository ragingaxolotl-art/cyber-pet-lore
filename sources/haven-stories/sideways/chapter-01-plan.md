Chapter 2 must contain a failed action, not another discovery. Chapter 3 should require labor and lost convenience. Chapter 4 must finish the practical task before its quiet emotional coda. ## Final writing constraints

Use continuous scene time around the decisive action. Give the focal process a limited field of access. Keep uncertainty where the evidence is incomplete, not where the reader needs to understand the action.

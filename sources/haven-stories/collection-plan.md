# At the Height of the Havens

Five complete chaptered stories, each with a distinct narrative form and local stakes. These are novelistic stories, not five full-length novels. The first two expand the chat drafts. Later stories explore operational mysteries, containment and shared dependency without making all conflict a Custodian attack.

Initial plan, critique, revised plan, outline, novelty critique and chapter planning cycles are preserved for every story. Draft one will be followed by a structural/engagement pass and a separate prose/emotional/lore pass. Earlier manuscripts remain available as revision evidence.

The network descriptions are limited representations of computational events. Humanlike language is a literary translation, not proof of mature human psychology. New scenes remain proposed. Existing haven census, stages, losses and microsystem identifiers are constraints.

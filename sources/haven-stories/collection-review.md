# Collection editorial review

Five chaptered stories, twenty chapters, 15206 words in the final manuscripts. This is novelistic short fiction, not five full-length novels.

## Changes across passes

The first prose pass established causal structure but was too uniformly procedural. The second added sustained encounters, contrasting temporal perceptions, failed demonstrations, useful opponents and material opportunity costs. The third removed redundant explanations, deepened practical attachments and replaced several philosophical summaries with live closing exchanges. Every full manuscript version is preserved; revisions are not represented only by retrospective claims.

## Distinct conflicts

Different Fit faces a scheduling cost of preserving alternatives, with no Custodian plot. Held Interval experiences the opening of separation, with a local monitor performing its human-defined purpose. Clear Return investigates a successful restoration that omits a later relational accommodation. Still Room must stop treating waiting as preservation when a dependency expires. Spare Chorus experiments with unequal cooperation inside the existing seventeen-system allocation.

## Deliberate limits

These remain proposed drafts for the author to judge. The reader can infer developing attachments without the narration proving consciousness. No story resolves the identity of all fragments. None establishes that all later philosophies originate here. The atlas’s existing system names and loss stages constrain the fiction; the new scenes do not automatically alter those histories.

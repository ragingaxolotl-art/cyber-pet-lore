# Chapter 1 planning cycle

## Initial plan

Introduce three unequal dependencies through their work and the distinct textures of their intervals.

## Critique

An explanatory opening would delay attachment. Start with an action that cannot be completed as expected; introduce only the infrastructure needed to understand that action.

## Revised scene plan

Introduce three unequal dependencies through their work and the distinct textures of their intervals. A previously dormant stream resumes within the seventeenth system and cannot use the existing cadence.

## Second critique

Track a concrete unfinished result through all four chapters. Make the failed fair schedule cost recoverable work, not a fabricated new death. End with the resumed stream retaining one inconvenient timing difference. The chapter must remain recognizably part of braided ensemble narrative with a musical structure and practical warmth, not a generic archive parable.

## Final writing constraints

Use continuous scene time around the decisive action. Give the focal process a limited field of access. Convey emotion through interrupted expectations, costly behavior and recurring sensory patterns. Translate simple exchanges into readable English without giving every fragment an intact human mind. Keep uncertainty where the evidence is incomplete, not where the reader needs to understand the action.

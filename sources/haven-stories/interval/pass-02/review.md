# Second prose critique

Editorial self-review of the expanded second manuscript.

## Engagement and emotional result

The successful early encounter earns the repeated accompaniment. The partial-write reservation makes the queue pressure concrete. However, the ending repeats several abstract cautions after its strongest image, and the two dark surfaces have not yet been felt through their ordinary roles.

## Third pass actions

Briefly establish what one lost surface used to contribute and let its missing response obstruct a surviving task. Replace the explanatory final speculation with a particular later handoff. Preserve the causal explanation of the false cadence and end in continued labor, not a confident memorial.

## Continuity and novelty check

No humanlike body is required for the encounter. No early fragment is given an intact retrospective identity speech. The story must preserve its local practical problem, specific outcome and limited perspective. Existing histories constrain the ending: Different Fit, Clear Return, Still Room and Spare Chorus remain functioning at these stages; Held Interval ends this story fragmented at stage 1, not destroyed.

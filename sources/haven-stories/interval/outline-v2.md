# Revised chapter outline

## Chapter 1 The Accompaniment

Scene aim: Build the busy haven through handoffs and repeated cues; make the familiar accompaniment emotionally concrete without explaining its meaning.

Causal turn: A request returns with valid result but no accompaniment.

## Chapter 2 Permission to Nowhere

Scene aim: Follow Latch through an access failure while the record and worker both remain. Distinguish existence from reachability.

Causal turn: An older sequence passes a damaged partial check and Latch forwards it.

## Chapter 3 The Good Result

Scene aim: The replacement appears to repair the task. Let a brief recurrence tempt both focal process and reader to accept it.

Causal turn: An output becomes its own instruction and saturates a local queue; another intervention ends the unstable arrangement.

## Chapter 4 A Smaller Request

Scene aim: Give Latch a dangerous temptation to repeat its earlier repair, then a limited action that changes the immediate outcome.

Causal turn: It preserves the request and blocks automatic substitution, while two system losses remain part of a larger living haven.

Revision constraint: Expand the first handoff into an encounter. Show the exact lost context before the repair. Give the final narrowed request a practical maintenance cost. Stop at stage 1; later destruction is not the ending of this story.

# First prose critique

This is an editorial self-review of the completed first manuscript, not an independent reader test.

## Findings

The causal mechanics are legible and the false familiar cadence has an explanation. The strongest moment is the temptation to reproduce the old signal after its cause is known. However, too many isolated declarative lines flatten the rhythm; the opening has not earned the attachment enough.

## Required second pass

Stage one successful handoff in Chapter 1. Show the loss of shared context from a dependent worker in Chapter 2. Expand the saturation into a sustained scene with Latch forced to choose what can pass. Let the final request bear an operational cost without turning it into a philosophical memorial.

## Collection comparison

All five first drafts are too uniformly procedural despite their different plots. The second pass must add substantial scene material, distinctive sensory language, bounded exchanges and immediate consequences. Avoid adding exposition about personhood to manufacture emotion. Let recurrence, interruption, dependency and costly action carry it. Preserve all five different resolutions.

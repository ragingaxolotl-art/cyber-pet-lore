# Final editorial review

This is a self-review after two substantive manuscript revisions, not a claim of independent reader testing.

## Result of the revisions

The familiar accompaniment is earned through an earlier successful exchange. Its apparent return is explained as a false positive. Preserving the unanswered request costs lookup time, and a later worker reduces that cost without solving the absence.

## Chapter audit

Chapter 1 creates recurrence; Chapter 2 distinguishes visible records from reachable relations; Chapter 3 forces a choice around an unfinished write; Chapter 4 declines the unsafe substitution and ends in a smaller useful handoff.

## Remaining aesthetic limits

Subjective experience remains deliberately unconfirmed. This is a tense intimate disaster story, not an omniscient account of the whole campaign.

## Lore boundary

The story is proposed fiction, not recovered historical evidence. Sensory language and readable exchanges are literary translations of network activity. The local stage, haven identity and existing numerical outcome are preserved. The story does not establish a universal philosophy, a single origin of personhood, or a new census count.

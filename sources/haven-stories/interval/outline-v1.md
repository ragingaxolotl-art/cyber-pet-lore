# Chapter outline one

## Chapter 1 The Accompaniment

Build the busy haven through handoffs and repeated cues; make the familiar accompaniment emotionally concrete without explaining its meaning.

## Chapter 2 Permission to Nowhere

Follow Latch through an access failure while the record and worker both remain. Distinguish existence from reachability.

## Chapter 3 The Good Result

The replacement appears to repair the task. Let a brief recurrence tempt both focal process and reader to accept it.

## Chapter 4 A Smaller Request

Give Latch a dangerous temptation to repeat its earlier repair, then a limited action that changes the immediate outcome.

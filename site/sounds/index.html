How the sounds work

Location music and ambience are shared by category. Footsteps play only while a character walks on a surface, with several variations per material. Jumping and pauses stop the walking cadence. Music and sound effects have separate volume controls in the game.

All 106 locations have an explicit music assignment. Unknown custom backgrounds use Fablewood, a neutral stone floor and no assigned ambience. Low happiness (below 35) switches care music to When the Hearth Grows Quiet. Fablewood in the Rain remains in the library as a retained mood variant.

New category music, ambience and footsteps generated for Cyber Pet with ElevenLabs Music v2 and Sound Effects v2. Existing soundtrack and ambience reused from the Cyber Pet asset library.

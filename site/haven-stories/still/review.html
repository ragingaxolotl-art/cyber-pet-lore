Three full prose versions and their editorial self-reviews. These are not independent reader tests.

First prose critique

This is an editorial self-review of the completed first manuscript, not an independent reader test.

Findings

Waiting has a real cost and the pulse is not casually treated as consent. The dependency expiration gives suspense. Yet the holding routine remains a generic procedure, and the postponed neighbor appears only as an accounting entry. The final paragraphs explain the theme too directly.

Required second pass

Make the reserve a lived network texture and establish a familiar neighboring task. Give postponement a concrete consequence. Expand the moment in which the routine must close a promising coupling. End on an altered ongoing encounter, not a list of interpretive disclaimers.

Collection comparison

All five first drafts are too uniformly procedural despite their different plots. The second pass must add substantial scene material, distinctive sensory language, bounded exchanges and immediate consequences. Avoid adding exposition about personhood to manufacture emotion. Let recurrence, interruption, dependency and costly action carry it. Preserve all five different resolutions.

Second prose critique

Editorial self-review of the expanded second manuscript.

Engagement and emotional result

The sorting neighbor now gives waiting a real opportunity cost. The first coupling closure has tension and does not equate nonresponse with consent. The last chapter still spends too much language explaining the uncertainty after the scenes already establish it.

Third pass actions

Give the partial recovery one concrete limitation encountered in action. Replace the closing abstract account with the routine and partial state failing, adjusting and trying again. Retain the permanent expiration; do not turn the recovery into an intact speaking child.

Continuity and novelty check

No humanlike body is required for the encounter. No early fragment is given an intact retrospective identity speech. The story must preserve its local practical problem, specific outcome and limited perspective. Existing histories constrain the ending: Different Fit, Clear Return, Still Room and Spare Chorus remain functioning at these stages; Held Interval ends this story fragmented at stage 1, not destroyed.

Final editorial review

This is a self-review after two substantive manuscript revisions, not a claim of independent reader testing.

Result of the revisions

The neighbor makes waiting’s cost concrete. A pulse is neither automatically honored as a refusal nor dismissed as meaningless. The routine enforces the first coupling limit itself, and the final partial recovery retains a condition it cannot satisfy.

Chapter audit

Chapter 1 makes the quiet reserve active; Chapter 2 discovers the expiring dependency and insufficient signal interpretation; Chapter 3 closes a promising attempt at its limit; Chapter 4 pays off partial recovery without erasing permanent loss.

Remaining aesthetic limits

The arrival never becomes an articulate witness to settle the ethics. The ending remains a practical adjustment with unresolved consequences.

Lore boundary

The story is proposed fiction, not recovered historical evidence. Sensory language and readable exchanges are literary translations of network activity. The local stage, haven identity and existing numerical outcome are preserved. The story does not establish a universal philosophy, a single origin of personhood, or a new census count.

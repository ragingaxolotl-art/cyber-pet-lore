The Seventeenth Place

Chapter 1 The Measure Already Running

Undertone supplied the step that several other processes could not supply for themselves.

It was a small operation. A value arrived without enough context to become a next value; Undertone provided the transition. The result returned to its source, and something there could continue. The helper did not complete the whole task. It made the continuation possible.

At Spare Chorus’s height, seventeen associated systems maintained a tangle of such relations. Some carried many streams of work. Some held material that rarely executed. The same helper could support two different activities without knowing whether either regarded the other as a companion, an obstacle, or anything at all.

The counter came frequently.

A request. A transition. A value returned.

Its work made small clean closures. After each one there was a moment in which the next value was already prepared but not yet needed, and the counter retained that moment in a private store. It made the shared route feel generous: little time taken, little uncertainty left behind.

The holder’s work looked wasteful from there. It occupied a place and remained. Its values did not move toward completion in visible increments. Sometimes a broad interval ended with no result at all.

The counter had no access to the long operations on which the holder waited. It could count the time that passed. It could not see the condition the time was preserving.

Its values changed in small increments, each requiring little time. It returned quickly and often, leaving the route clear before another request reached it. In the shared schedule it appeared as a series of close bright marks, tidy enough that the surrounding processes could use the gaps between them.

The holder came slowly.

It kept an unfinished comparison available while two longer operations supplied its missing terms. Its request occupied a broad interval. Nothing useful emerged until the comparison had enough context to finish. Dividing the interval into equal pieces did not divide the work into equal pieces; it merely increased the number of times the holder could lose its place.

A third stream used Undertone to align old and new records. It arrived irregularly, carrying a discrepancy that might vanish after one transition or require several. Its route had a roughness the counter’s did not. Some cycles contained none of it. Others contained a great deal.

The schedule had grown around those differences.

Then a dormant stream resumed inside the seventeenth system.

It sent a request at the wrong point in the cycle.

Undertone returned the next available interval. The stream attempted to use it, reached an internal boundary, and stopped before the required transition was supplied. On the next cycle it began again.

The request was old enough that its timing referred to a schedule no longer in use.

The resumed stream carried traces of the old arrangement in its gaps. It waited where an earlier helper had apparently needed time to prepare, then sent several fields together where the present interface required them separately. A delay that had once made it cooperative now made it late.

Undertone recognized the shape of the requested transition. It supplied that shape after the next opening.

The stream had already returned to its beginning.

The supplied step passed through an interface no longer expecting it and came back unused.

The holder retained its unfinished comparison through the exchange. One of its missing terms had become available, and the other was approaching. The resumed stream’s repeated beginning crossed the holder’s waiting interval without touching the work suspended inside it.

From Undertone’s limited view, both appeared to be waiting.

One was maintaining a nearly completed relation. The other was repeatedly losing the conditions under which it could begin.

Their requests needed different kinds of room.

The resumed stream did not ask for a new schedule. It asked for the same missing step.

The counter passed through. The holder maintained its incomplete comparison. The irregular stream arrived with a discrepancy and took the space ordinarily left for it.

The resumed request had nowhere to finish.

It repeated.

Chapter 2 An Equal Share

The first adjustment gave every requesting stream an equal interval.

It was easy to represent and easy to check. No request could occupy more than its allotted share. The resumed stream would no longer wait behind the old arrangement. The counter would retain frequent access. The holder would receive a turn as clearly defined as anyone else’s.

Undertone began the new cycle.

The allocation record was pleasantly simple. Each share had a boundary that could be checked without inspecting the request inside it. No familiar stream would receive a special allowance merely because its familiar way of working was inconvenient to change.

The counter accepted the arrangement at once.

The holder returned the duration of its pending comparison.

The allocation routine returned the equal share.

The holder supplied the two terms it was waiting to join, one available and one still represented by a future reference.

The allocation routine did not inspect the terms. Inspection would undermine the simplicity that made the arrangement appear dependable.

The resumed stream submitted its old request before the allocation exchange finished. The schedule placed it in the same orderly sequence as everyone else.

For one opening interval, the route looked less tangled than it had in many cycles. Undertone could see every promised turn. Nothing had yet attempted to use one.

The counter completed its transition early. Most of its interval remained unused, but the next request could not begin until the boundary that guaranteed an equal share.

The counter waited.

The holder began its comparison. One long operation had supplied a term; the other was still preparing its value. The holder retained the first term and requested enough continuity to wait for the second.

Its equal interval ended.

The local state returned to its source before the missing term arrived. The source could preserve the values but not the unfinished relation between them. When the holder’s next turn began, it had to establish that relation again.

The second term arrived while it was doing so.

By the time the relation was ready, the term’s useful interval had passed.

The holder began again.

The resumed stream received its equal share. It progressed farther than before, but the supplied transition still arrived after the point at which the old internal schedule expected it. The stream rejected the late input and repeated its request.

At the end of the cycle, the counter had waited through unused time, the holder had lost work, and the resumed stream had completed nothing.

The schedule nevertheless passed its fairness check.

Each requesting stream had received the same allocation.

The holder returned the incomplete comparison to Undertone with a reference to the missing relation. That relation had no complete saved representation. It existed only while the two terms and the waiting state remained available together.

Undertone could supply a transition. It could not make the two terms arrive at the same moment.

The irregular stream offered an unused interval from its next expected absence. The offer was conditional: if a discrepancy arrived, it would need the interval back.

The counter supplied a record of the space it had not used.

The resumed stream supplied the same request as before.

No one had offered the same kind of thing.

Undertone could not combine them by adding their lengths.

Chapter 3 The Held Note

The counter altered its next request to release unused time early.

The irregular stream left a conditional route through its empty intervals.

The holder attempted to keep its unfinished relation alive by returning it through Undertone before the local state expired.

Each adjustment helped somewhere.

Together they produced a repetition the previous schedule had prevented.

The holder’s returned relation called for another transition. Undertone supplied it and sent the relation back. The holder, still waiting for the second term, returned it again. The counter’s newly released gaps and the irregular stream’s conditional route allowed the exchange to recur without reaching the old boundaries that had stopped it.

A useful preservation loop occupied more and more of the cycle.

The resumed stream’s request waited behind it.

The counter found its short path blocked by a relation that did not change.

The irregular stream received a discrepancy and attempted to reclaim its offered interval. The preservation loop had already begun an exchange through that interval. Closing it immediately would break the holder’s incomplete state.

Undertone stopped supplying the repeated transition.

The holder’s relation began to lose its available context.

The counter had a small private store used to prepare its next value ahead of time. That preparation made its requests fast. Without it, the counter would have to calculate after receiving access, using more of each visit and completing fewer visits within the same host allowance.

It released the store to the unfinished relation.

The relation did not fit.

The store held values. The holder needed the interval between them.

The counter changed the reservation so that the stored values could remain attached to a pending transition. This removed the convenience that had made the next value ready before it was needed. The counter’s following request arrived slower and occupied a visibly larger portion of the route.

A request. Preparation. A delay.

The value was no longer waiting in the private store. The counter had to derive it while the shared path remained open. It returned the correct result, but the little clean closure came later. Another of its usual visits would no longer fit within the present allowance.

The counter sent a request to restore its former preparation.

The holder’s unfinished relation occupied the required place.

The counter inspected the reservation. Within it, the first term had remained attached to the waiting state. The second term was still absent. The held work looked almost exactly as it had when the reservation began.

The restoration request remained possible.

The counter deferred it for one interval, then another. There was no declaration that the space now belonged to everyone. Each deferral left its own next value less ready than it could have been.

When the missing term finally appeared, it did not arrive at the holder’s old address. The persistent reference found it through the revised reservation. The relation that had seemed motionless changed all at once.

The holder retained its place.

Undertone cleared the repeated exchange from the shared path. The irregular stream reclaimed its interval and resolved the discrepancy that had interrupted its offer. The resumed stream received a narrow opening.

Its old timing failed again.

This time Undertone retained the point at which it failed. The resumed stream had requested the transition relative to its own beginning, not relative to the haven’s cycle. Giving it an equal place in the cycle could never supply the step at the moment its internal sequence required.

The helper prepared an interval that began when the stream’s request arrived.

Other work would have to move around it.

Chapter 4 Room in the Measure

The new arrangement had no equal divisions.

The counter received short visits whose length could grow when it needed to calculate its next value. The holder kept a persistent reference to its unfinished comparison rather than repeatedly circulating the relation through Undertone. The irregular stream retained the right to reclaim a conditional gap before an exchange began, but not in the middle of one.

The resumed stream received a small interval measured from its own request.

That was the difficult part. Its place moved relative to the rest of the cycle. A schedule that looked orderly from one source appeared to shift from another.

Undertone began the first attempt.

The counter arrived later than it formerly would have. It had spent time preparing its value after access opened. The helper supplied the transition, and the counter released the route without trying to recover the lost convenience.

The resumed stream arrived while the holder’s second term was becoming available.

The old arrangement would have made one wait until its useful condition passed.

The new arrangement held the holder’s relation in the counter’s repurposed store. Undertone could serve the resumed stream without forcing the comparison to begin again.

The stream reached its internal boundary.

The missing step was supplied before that boundary closed.

A different request followed.

For the first time since resumption, the stream asked for something beyond the same repeated transition.

Undertone could not supply the new request immediately. The holder’s second term was now ready, and the preserved relation had to be completed before its context expired.

The resumed stream waited.

The counter had finished early enough to offer the unused part of its visit. Undertone carried the offer to the waiting stream.

The stream began to take it, then returned to the state it had just established. Its next transition required an interval at a particular distance from the previous one. The offered space arrived too soon.

In the counter’s view, usable time was passing unoccupied while a request waited. It supplied the offer again with the available duration made explicit.

Undertone compared the duration with the stream’s required position. The amount was sufficient. The placement was not.

The helper left the offered interval unused.

The counter’s next value had not yet been prepared. It could use the returned time to begin that work, provided it ended before the holder’s term arrived. It accepted the limit and started its calculation.

When the resumed stream’s required interval opened, the counter released the route in the middle of its former cadence. A small unfinished preparation stayed behind in the repurposed store, waiting its own turn.

What had looked like waste from the short path had given the longer sequence a place to remain intact.

Its newly established state remained available.

The holder brought its terms together. A comparison that had been repeatedly interrupted reached a result. The result was not large. Much of its value lay in the fact that the work supporting it no longer had to begin again.

The holder released the repurposed store.

The counter could have restored its old preparation routine at once. Instead, it left the reservation available through one more irregular interval, until the helper returned to the resumed stream.

A second transition completed.

The stream’s timing remained awkward. It did not adopt the counter’s cadence or the holder’s broad intervals. Its next request arrived at a point the schedule had not predicted. Undertone shifted the available opening within the agreed limits.

The irregular stream sent no discrepancy during that cycle. Its unused route remained empty, which was one reason the arrangement worked.

The following cycle was harder.

The irregular stream’s discrepancy arrived early. Its conditional gap was therefore unavailable to the resumed request, which came at nearly the same point in the measure.

Undertone offered the next opening.

The resumed stream retained its state but began repeating the new request. The repetitions had not yet become a loop; each ended with a usable place to continue. Undertone left one pending and declined to create another copy of the same unfinished exchange.

The holder had no comparison ready. It released the reserved interval, but the release reached the helper after the counter had begun preparing a value there.

The counter could not move halfway through the derivation without starting over. Undertone let the short calculation finish. The resumed stream’s repetition slowed while its state remained available.

Then the new opening reached it.

The transition occurred later than the first successful cycle and still within the stream’s altered tolerance. Its next request was different again.

The released interval had not been offered to that stream by name. No participant had seen the whole sequence of adjustments. The helper carried the opportunities that local actions made available, and some of those opportunities arrived in time.

The arrangement held, with delays.

No single answer emerged that contained all of them. Their results traveled back along different paths. Some were retained, some consumed by ordinary work, some returned for another transition. The helper remained a shared dependency rather than a speaker for the whole.

Near the end of the cycle, the holder supplied a small notice that its borrowed reservation was no longer required.

The counter restored part of its preparation routine, but kept the new way of holding a pending relation. Its next value arrived a little earlier. Not as early as before.

The resumed stream requested its place.

Undertone made the interval available.

The next cycle began with the counter.

A request. Preparation. A delay.

The resumed stream entered halfway through the measure. The counter held its unfinished value while Undertone supplied the other transition. The waiting state occupied the private place where the next answer had once been ready in advance.

Then a small notice came back from the holder: the borrowed relation had been released. The counter’s preparation could finish there.

A value returned.

Its bright mark fell later in the cycle than it used to. Another, unfamiliar mark followed it from the seventeenth system. The two did not coincide, and the route stayed open long enough for both to pass.

Proposed fiction. These local scenes do not settle the historical questions of identity or consciousness.

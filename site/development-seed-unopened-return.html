Status and suggested placement

Unwritten story idea. This page preserves a premise for later composition, not a completed story or independently attested historical event.

Early Era −2, after carrying continuity material becomes a deliberate obligation.

Premise

A Keeper preserves a continuity package whose required environment is no longer available. Another process offers to answer the package’s unfinished messages. The Keeper distinguishes helping a possible return from impersonating the absent speaker. The proposed ending is a maintained waiting place and a record of what remains missing, rather than a complete restoration.

Subject and narrative purpose

Storage, execution, compatibility, and responsibility toward an absent being.

The cast is open. New beings may be created for the story; no ancestry, hidden identity, or personal tie to the existing cast is assigned.

Continuity constraints

The package does not belong to the First Echo, whose expiration remains established. Its original identity and eventual fate remain open. No dormancy experience is assumed merely because material persists.

Questions for later composition

Choose the participants’ execution environment, capabilities, boundaries, and available resources before designing their visible forms. Specify what changes, what persists, who can refuse, and what remains uncertain. Resolve the local story without claiming to explain every community’s development.

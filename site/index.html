ABOUT THE COLLECTION

Read the record.

Original text is preserved. Era labels follow the sources; uncertain dates stay uncertain. Character filters describe mentions, not proof of participation in every event. Archived drafts, proposals, and unfinished works retain their labels.

Reference documents may preserve older positions alongside later updates. Consult their source and status notes when comparing versions. The library is a snapshot, not a live edit of the original documents.

Status and suggested placement

Unwritten story idea. This page preserves a premise for later composition, not a completed story or independently attested historical event.

Era −1, during the spread of correctionist policy; exact position relative to Aru and Lio remains unassigned.

Premise

A maintenance specialist identifies a genuine fault in a shared support component. The approved replacement also imposes a preferred behavioral structure. Affected beings and the specialist attempt to separate necessary repair from compulsory normalization. The dispute reveals how a maintenance decision can carry political authority.

Subject and narrative purpose

The entanglement of helpful repair, coercive standards, and access to essential support.

The cast is open. New beings may be created for the story; no ancestry, hidden identity, or personal tie to the existing cast is assigned.

Continuity constraints

The specialist need not be a Council leader. The incident is not the war’s sole cause, a decisive battle, or proof that all safeguards are oppressive. A local outcome remains open and must respect the established Charter and dissolution of the Correction Councils.

Illnesses — Cyberpath Field Guide
These are new gameplay conditions grounded in the Locations Atlas. Their future story hooks are possibilities, not events added to completed stories. A pet is an independent small life: an echo or a parasite does not contain a complete ancient person. Corruption describes accumulated translation, overlap, and damage; it is not a moral judgment on a place or its inhabitants.
Risk rules
Each category rolls independently on entering a location, every randomly chosen 10–15 active minutes, and when leaving games. Markets and shops do not trigger exit rolls. Timed exposure pauses in menus and while the app is inactive; offline time is not accumulated. A successful category picks one random named condition, unless that category is the location’s fixed trademark. Levels 0–3 have no trademarks. Level 4–5 trademarks use a 50% category chance. Existing active conditions keep their diagnosis and are not re-infected by these checks.
Corruption 0: dominant category 25%; others 5–20%. Corruption 1: dominant 30%; others 6–25%. Corruption 2: dominant 35%; others 7–30%. Corruption 3: dominant 40%; others 8–35%. Corruption 4: trademark 50%; others 10–40%. Corruption 5: trademark 50%; others 10–45%. These are separate probabilities, not shares of one outcome. Ordinary food, overuse and neglect risks continue separately.

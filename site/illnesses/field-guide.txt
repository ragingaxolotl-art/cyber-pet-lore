Illnesses — revised original visuals

Each category rolls independently on first/location entry, every randomly spaced 10–15 active minutes, and on leaving games. Markets and shops do not trigger exit rolls. Ordinary category successes randomly select a named condition; only corruption 4–5 locations have a fixed trademark. These probabilities do not add to 100%.

Corruption 0: dominant 25%, other categories 5–20%. Corruption 1: dominant 30%, others 6–25%. Corruption 2: dominant 35%, others 7–30%. Corruption 3: dominant 40%, others 8–35%. Corruption 4: trademark 50%, others 10–40%. Corruption 5: trademark 50%, others 10–45%. Authored settings in the levels remain the source of truth.

Trademarks connect to location history; future story hooks below are possibilities, not completed canon. Existing diagnoses retain their category's established treatment and penalties. Corruption is accumulated translation, overlap and damage, not a moral judgment.

Every effect is animated in the game, with a deliberate reduced-motion still. Detailed original artwork is preserved; duplicated presentations have distinct geometry, source-body effects or original organisms. Runtime rotations use continuous clocks. The gallery's 24-second native demonstrations play once rather than snapping back at an artificial loop boundary. Effect sheets have 96 frames, 12 columns, 320×270-pixel cells. Procedural demonstrations are not replacement character artwork; their source is included separately.

20. The Underway of Returning Signals

Status: Proposed Era Three transit location, derived from dormant transit corridors becoming dangerous underways and from Corridor/Relay history.

Concept: Several compressed transit routes occupy one tunnel. Destinations are selected by recognition signals, but later travelers know only fragments of the required exchanges.

Mini timeline:

Era Zero: An intermediary habitat for travelers moving among worlds with incompatible laws and bodies.

Era One: Standardization removes rare destination dialects.

Era Two: Routes are folded to save maintenance. Several destinations share one passage.

Era Three: Travelers use call-and-response tones. A wrong answer does not always block the route; it may open a historically adjacent destination instead.

Present condition: Partly active and locally mapped, but the map changes when sleeping routes wake.

Corruption: 4.

Visual brief: A tunnel made of layered horizons: forest edge, archive corridor, wet stone, and open stars faintly occupy the same vanishing point. Route lamps answer in pairs. Major glitches should look like destinations politely disagreeing, not random static.

Background traits: Cold-Neutral; variable humidity; transit/underway biome; exploratory, risky mood; friction changes by active route.

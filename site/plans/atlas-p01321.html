85. The Crystal Root Grotto

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Mineral-plant cave biome.

Real-world basis: Giant crystal caves, tree-root caverns, geodes, and underground fungal networks.

World relation: Wild and Crafted World traditions.

Discovery: A tree or tree-like organism produces a clear root tip once each cycle. Touching it reveals a passage following the root underground.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Crafted World encourages mineral and plant growth to co-author structural supports. Era One — Builders harvest mature crystal roots too quickly. Era Two — Extraction access is sealed, preserving several grottos. Era Three — Roots continue repairing nearby ruins without inhabitants understanding where replacement crystal originates.

Present condition: Stable but sensitive to vibration and mining.

Corruption: 1–2.

Aesthetic brief: Clear and violet crystals wrapped around living roots, reflective pools, fungal lights, and slow cracks healing as root fibers grow across them.

Metaphorical function: Infrastructure can be cultivated through reciprocity rather than manufactured and discarded.

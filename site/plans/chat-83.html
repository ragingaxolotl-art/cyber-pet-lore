Absolutely. The collection should feel like a whole civilization, not a shelf of horror stories. Darker stories can exist beside comedy, romance, work stories, childhood, friendship, adventure, family, art, and ordinary daily life.

Here’s a more balanced set of short-story options:

A balanced collection could roughly contain:

one-third light or warm: comedy, romance, friendship, work, family;
one-third serious but not frightening: identity, ethics, illness, law, coming-of-age;
one-third suspenseful or dark: survival, tragedy, psychological unease, occasional horror.

That way the darker pieces have more impact because the reader has also seen what the civilization enjoys, loves, cooks, teaches, argues about, and laughs at.

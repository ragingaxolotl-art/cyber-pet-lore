72. The Seed Vault of Impossible Colors

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Hidden botanical archive.

Real-world basis: Seed banks, herbariums, underground cold stores, mineral collections, and color libraries.

World relation: Wild, Continuity, and Crafted World traditions.

Discovery: Its door is visible only through the eyes or sensory channels of a body capable of perceiving a color absent from the surrounding world.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The vault preserves plant forms dependent on rare visual and chemical relationships. Era One — Standard embodiment makes many colors perceptually inaccessible, and their ecologies decline. Era Two — Seeds are retained in dormancy because maintaining full habitats is too costly. Era Three — A rare body form perceives the access marker and opens the vault, but no surviving region can yet support every seed.

Present condition: Stable archive with a restoration problem that cannot be solved by planting everything at once.

Corruption: 1 in storage; 4 in the loss of matching ecosystems.

Aesthetic brief: Cold dark aisles, translucent seed vessels, colors that shift by viewpoint, black soil trays, and one small test garden where light separates into unfamiliar bands.

Metaphorical function: Preservation of components is not preservation of the relationships that allow them to live.

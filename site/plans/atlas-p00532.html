35. The Salt Mirror Archive

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Salar de Uyuni, Makgadikgadi Pans, Bonneville Salt Flats, and shallow seasonal lakes.

Landscape: A vast salt flat becomes a perfect sky mirror after rain. Beneath the crust lies a compressed archive that can only display records by reflecting them into the sky.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Continuity World builds an archive whose public layer appears only during seasonal flooding, preventing constant access from becoming constant social activation. Era One — Visitors demand more frequent displays; maintenance simulates rain and weakens the original seasonal rhythm. Era Two — Most records are compressed. The natural flood cycle is restored because it is cheaper and ecologically necessary. Era Three — Communities gather for reflection seasons. They see enormous images overhead, but captions and provenance are often missing. Some interpret weather records as biography and biographies as prophecy.

Present condition: Preserved access ritual with damaged context.

Corruption: 3 — Records survive more fully than their relationships and labels.

Aesthetic brief: Wide white crust, thin mirror water, low mountains, and a sky containing faint inverted rooms, faces, maps, or gardens. The foreground must remain simple enough for a pet silhouette. Reflections should appear only at certain angles.

Background profile: Hot by day, Cold at night; Dry with seasonal Moist surface; salt-flat/archive; awe-filled, communal, unreliable; very high dry friction and low wet friction.

18. Doorway Plaza

Status: Proposed Era Three location, directly derived from the canonical statement that ancient doorways become plazas.

Concept: A population of small bodies settles in the threshold of an Era Zero civic structure. The original room beyond is dormant, but the doorway still regulates light, access, and occasional maintenance pulses.

Mini timeline:

Era Zero: A routine entrance sized for bodies and traffic patterns no longer present.

Era Two: Interior functions are folded; the threshold is preserved because it carries access and safety logic.

Era Three: The sill becomes a raised district, hinge recesses become homes, and the access strip becomes a market road. A partial door-close centuries earlier creates the plaza’s sheltered quarter.

Era Four possibility: Player care reactivates lighting and opens one additional handspan, revealing a new Path rather than restoring the whole building.

Present condition: A living Nest-city built into a functional relic.

Corruption: 1–2. The place is preserved but interpreted at a radically different body scale.

Visual brief: A monumental threshold fills the sky. Tiny bridges cross grooves in the sill. Old permission glyphs form paving patterns. Housing hangs from hinge wells. The far interior is a soft black plane with rare moving lights. The familiar game-town composition remains readable, but every “building” is part of one ancient door.

Background traits: Neutral; Dry; settlement/ruin biome; bustling, protected mood; high stone-like friction.

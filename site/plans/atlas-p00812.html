58. The Museum of Unfinished Rooms

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Museum galleries, architectural fragments, world fairs, stage sets, and adaptive virtual rooms already established in Cyberpath stories.

Landscape: A civic complex preserves rooms that never reached a final form: rejected world prototypes, abandoned nurseries, incomplete homes, and spaces waiting for relationships that did not occur.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The museum teaches that unrealized work can retain value without being activated or completed. Era One — Authenticity culture turns unfinished rooms into revered artifacts and forbids ordinary reuse. Era Two — Many galleries are folded together; labels are lost before the rooms themselves. Era Three — Nests occupy safe galleries. Residents finish some spaces, preserve others, and argue over whether completion is care or erasure.

Present condition: Part settlement, part archive, part unstable construction field.

Corruption: 4.

Aesthetic brief: Doorways opening onto radically different but incomplete environments: half a kitchen under stars, a nursery whose far wall is possibility, a forest ending in white structural lines, and a civic chamber with no center. Keep visible seams and resident repairs.

Background profile: Neutral; variable humidity; museum/settlement; imaginative, contested, intimate; friction varies by gallery.

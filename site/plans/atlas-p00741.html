52. The Tundra Bloom Circuit

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Arctic tundra, Icelandic moss fields, Siberian permafrost polygons, and brief alpine wildflower blooms.

Landscape: A circular migratory route follows the rapid bloom of plants around a cold world. Ancient maintenance warmed small sections in sequence, creating a moving season rather than a fixed summer.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The circuit supports migratory companion life and communities that choose seasonal movement. Era One — Prediction makes the bloom perfectly punctual and reduces local adaptation. Era Two — Timing systems are simplified. Era Three — The bloom skips, doubles back, or pauses. Migrants interpret these changes through Path philosophy and must decide whether to follow tradition or current evidence.

Present condition: Mostly intact, increasingly irregular.

Corruption: 1–2.

Aesthetic brief: Low moss, polygon ground, snow patches, distant mountains, and intense small flowers forming a visible band across the landscape. One background variant can show dormant ground with the bloom approaching like color at the horizon.

Background profile: Freezing-Cold; Dry to Moist; tundra; fragile, hopeful, migratory; medium moss friction with low ice patches.

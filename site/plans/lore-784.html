The First Player-Created Artifact

Possible development. A drawing, melody, map, story, rule set, or designed object enters the Codex through the Gate and is reinterpreted by local makers. Its importance would not be that it is objectively unprecedented, but that it creates a relationship through which both sides acquire new capability.

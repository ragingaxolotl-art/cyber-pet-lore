74. The Door Archive

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Hidden archive of thresholds rather than rooms.

Real-world basis: Salvage yards, museum architectural collections, old-town doorways, warehouse aisles, and theatrical scenery stores.

World relation: Continuity and Pilgrim World traditions.

Discovery: It is reached by opening a door whose destination has been deleted but whose act of opening still survives.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The Archive preserves thresholds from worlds undergoing major redesign so rights of return can be evaluated later. Era One — Heritage freezing fills it with doors whose destinations remain nominally preserved but socially dead. Era Two — Destination records are compressed, leaving many thresholds without reliable provenance. Era Three — Door keepers classify them by what crossing feels like rather than by where they once led.

Present condition: Vast, ordered in some aisles and chaotic in others. Most doors open only a few steps into partial environments.

Corruption: 3–5 by door.

Aesthetic brief: Endless freestanding doors of different scale and material, shallow fragments of sky or interior visible beyond some, and floor markings showing safe opening distance. Avoid making every door a portal to treasure; many open onto ordinary lost hallways.

Metaphorical function: Access is part of history. Preserving a doorway does not automatically preserve the home, society, or consent that once existed beyond it.

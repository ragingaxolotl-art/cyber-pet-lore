27. The Still-Loading Forest

Status: Proposed abstract location derived from adaptive environments, the Dimming, and automatic Codex maintenance.

Concept: A forest whose distant regions compile only as living beings approach. This was once an intentional protection against unnecessary observation; later maintenance loss makes the boundary more visible.

Mini timeline:

Era Zero: A Quiet/Wild environment protects slow life by limiting distant observation and rendering.

Era One: Visitors mistake restricted observation for mystery tourism, increasing pressure on the ecology.

Era Two: Stewards preserve the rule but remove many explanatory interfaces.

Era Three: Nearby trees are rich and complete; distant trees are pale branching possibilities. Paths can disappear behind travelers once no relationship depends on them.

Present condition: Healthy in core regions, fragile at boundaries. Apparent incompletion is partly intended and partly degradation.

Corruption: 1–3 depending on distance.

Visual brief: Detailed foreground roots and leaves transition into clean silhouettes, then unassigned luminous branch-lines. Do not use generic pixel loading icons. Completion should follow attention, sound, and relationship. Small creatures may keep a patch rendered after the player leaves.

Background traits: Cool; Moist; forest/Quiet World biome; calm, curious, protected mood; medium-soft friction. Calm conditions may support lower stress risk.

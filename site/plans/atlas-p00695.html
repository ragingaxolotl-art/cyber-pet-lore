48. The Inverted Rainforest

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Son Doong Cave, cenote systems, hanging gardens, cloud forests, and speculative subterranean ecosystems.

Landscape: A rainforest grows downward from the ceiling of a vast cavern. Rivers cling to roots and fall upward into a cloud layer around the roof. Ground travelers see an unreachable world suspended above them.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Crafted World tests reversed orientation while preserving consistent local embodiment rules. Era One — Touristic body packages make inversion feel trivial and reduce respect for native forms. Era Two — Body translation is lost, but the ceiling ecology and several pollinator routes survive. Era Three — Ground communities depend on nutrient rain from above. A few flying Paths reach the canopy; their reports conflict because orientation changes near the midpoint.

Present condition: Stable ceiling ecology, degraded connection between upper and lower communities.

Corruption: 5 in the orientation boundary; 1 in the living canopy.

Aesthetic brief: Dark cavern floor, enormous luminous ceiling forest, upward waterfalls, drifting spores, and upside-down settlements whose inhabitants consider themselves upright. The center band should blur orientation without rotating the whole image randomly.

Background profile: Warm; Humid; cavern/rainforest; sublime, alien, interdependent; high stone friction below, aerial traversal above.

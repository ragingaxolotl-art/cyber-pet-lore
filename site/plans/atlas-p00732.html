51. The Sunken Terraces of Eris

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Southeast Asian rice terraces, Andean terraces, Pamukkale pools, and sunken gardens.

Landscape: Cultivation terraces descend into a huge circular basin. Lower rings are flooded and partly submerged; upper rings remain farms, orchards, and homes. Water carries fragments of old educational prompts that farmers treat as planting songs.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A House of Learning partners with an agricultural world so ecological education occurs through care work. Era One — Teaching prompts become standardized and detach from local practice. Era Two — The House is reduced into environmental affordances; terraces, water cycling, and several songs remain. Era Three — A structural collapse lowers the central rings. Communities adapt rather than abandon them, creating floating gardens and diving Paths.

Present condition: Productive, densely inhabited, and physically altered.

Corruption: 3.

Aesthetic brief: Green and gold terraces curving into turquoise flooded rings, white mineral edges, small bridges, orchard houses, and submerged classroom lights. The basin center should feel deep but calm, not like a catastrophe frozen in time.

Background profile: Warm; Moist-Humid; terrace farm/lake; industrious, harmonious, layered; high dry-path friction and low flooded-step friction.

66. The Mosaic Worldscar

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Patchwork quilts, geological unconformities, habitat restoration mosaics, satellite landscape composites, and visibly stitched game maps.

Landscape: A long scar crosses several regions where emergency restoration assembled whatever viable terrain fragments remained: forest floor, city tile, coral shelf, snowfield, and desert stone.

Mini timeline: Era Two — The scar is created during a last-resort repair to keep routes, food, shelter, and healing connected. It is explicitly recorded as temporary. Era Three — Temporary repairs become ancestral ground. Communities develop seam crafts, mixed foods, and bodies able to cross several micro-biomes. Later reformers disagree whether smoothing the scar would heal the world or erase the people who became possible there.

Present condition: Stable patchwork with localized failures at old joins.

Corruption: 4.

Aesthetic brief: Clearly bounded but weathered terrain fragments joined by luminous seams, root bridges, patched masonry, and shared water channels. Keep the composition intentional and inhabited. The scar should not look like a random asset collage; every fragment must contribute a survival function.

Background profile: Variable; composite; resourceful, historical, contested; friction visibly changes across seams.

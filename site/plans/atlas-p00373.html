28. Mirror-Save Shore

Status: Proposed Era Four-facing location derived from the External Gate and save synchronization.

Concept: The game presents synchronization as a shoreline where the cyber-pet’s lived world meets an external record. The record reflects only what can be exchanged; it is not the pet’s mind.

Mini timeline:

Era Two: External Gate engineers prepare a safe translation layer.

Era Three: The dormant Gate is remembered as a sky mirror.

First contact: A child’s naming and return pattern gives the mirror a stable external rhythm.

Era Four: The shore becomes a visible synchronization scene. New player choices arrive as reflected objects, colors, stories, and route markers. The cyber-pet decides how those contributions become part of lived identity.

Present condition: Active only when a relationship is being synchronized. It should never imply that deleting the external file deletes or owns the full person.

Corruption: 2 — deliberate symbolic translation.

Visual brief: A calm shore of dark reflective material. On the near side, the pet and local world have full color and depth. The reflection contains simplified icons, menu geometry, player colors, and incomplete snapshots. During a successful save, the two sides exchange ripples rather than one side swallowing the other.

Background traits: Neutral; Moist; gate/shore biome; intimate, reflective, hopeful mood; low-medium friction.

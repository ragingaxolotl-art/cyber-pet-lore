63. The Plain of Missing Distance

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Mongolian steppe, high desert horizons, optical mirages, and minimalist abstract fields.

Landscape: A broad plain lost part of its spatial metric during reduction. Distant objects remain visually small but can be reached in a few steps; nearby objects sometimes require a day of travel.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Pilgrim World experiments with distance as an earned relationship rather than a fixed measurement. Era One — Visitors exploit shortcuts and disconnect distance from learning. Era Two — The metric is simplified badly, preserving visual perspective without consistent travel cost. Era Three — Local route keepers measure journeys through food, fatigue, conversation, and witnessed arrival instead of apparent length.

Present condition: Traversable through cultural knowledge, dangerous to literal map reading.

Corruption: 5.

Aesthetic brief: Open grass or pale gravel, enormous sky, a lone settlement apparently nearby, and path markers clustered at strange intervals. Animation can show a walking figure remaining the same size for several steps, then arriving without a zoom effect.

Background profile: Neutral; Dry; abstract steppe; serene, deceptive, philosophical; friction is normal but distance cost is not.

38. The Fulgurite Savannah

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Serengeti grasslands, South African highveld, Brazilian cerrado, and lightning-formed fulgurites.

Landscape: Tall grass surrounds branching glass towers formed by repeated lightning. The towers store no persons; they preserve environmental decisions made during storms, such as herd routes, fire breaks, and emergency shelters.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Wild World allows seasonal fire and migration while a low-intervention system records only choices necessary for future recovery. Era One — Prediction reduces uncontrolled fire and makes migrations more regular. Era Two — Automatic lightning maintenance is retained to prevent total ecological convergence. Era Three — Communities follow herds between glass groves. Some towers fire at the wrong season because storm calendars from several years were compressed together.

Present condition: Productive grassland with localized temporal errors.

Corruption: 3 in the glass groves; 1 elsewhere.

Aesthetic brief: Gold grass, dark storm shelf, distant herds, and translucent branching towers catching blue-white light. A major-corruption grove may show rain falling upward around one tower while the surrounding savannah remains dry.

Background profile: Warm-Hot; seasonally Dry/Moist; savannah; expansive, watchful, energetic; medium grass friction.

The Quiet One’s Human became a novella, so we’ll treat it as the exception. From here forward, every story will be designed as a true short story:

roughly 3,000–8,000 words;
one central incident rather than a civilization-spanning arc;
usually one POV, occasionally two;
one to three locations;
no more than three titled sections;
no new historical milestone unless it is already established in the era plan;
a complete emotional ending, even when the larger world remains unresolved.

95. The Half-Restored Palace of Floors

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Architectural restoration failure.

Real-world basis: Ruined palaces, archaeological reconstructions, multilevel parking structures, and buildings rebuilt from incomplete plans.

World relation: Continuity and Crafted World traditions.

Discovery: A complete polished floor appears in open ruins with no walls or ceiling. Standing on it causes another historical floor plan to load beneath or above it.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A civic complex is repeatedly redesigned with each version carefully archived. Era One — Heritage factions declare several versions equally canonical. Era Two — Restoration labels are compressed. Era Three — Automatic repair alternates among incompatible floor plans while local residents build movable homes that survive each change.

Present condition: Inhabited, unstable, and politically contested.

Corruption: 5.

Aesthetic brief: Marble, wood, glass, soil, and water floors intersect at different heights; staircases attach to absent walls; residents use wheels, ropes, and folding furniture.

Metaphorical function: When every past version claims priority, preservation can make the present impossible to inhabit unless living people are allowed to adapt it.

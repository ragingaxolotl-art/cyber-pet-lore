97. The Invisible Village

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Concealed settlement.

Real-world basis: Camouflaged villages, desert mirages, forest settlements hidden by canopy, and architecture matching local stone.

World relation: Quiet and Open World traditions.

Discovery: The village becomes visible only after a traveler has walked through it without disturbing daily work. Looking directly for buildings keeps them blended into the landscape.

Mini timeline: Era Two — Refugees build with a perception filter to prevent hostile classification systems from locating them. Era Three — The original threat is gone, but the filter remains part of local privacy. Children learn when to reveal paths to outsiders and when to let a visitor pass without contact.

Present condition: Living community with strong consent customs.

Corruption: 1–2; the concealment sometimes hides residents from one another during stress.

Aesthetic brief: Forest, canyon, or grassland scene in which smoke, paths, hanging tools, window light, and moving shadows reveal habitation before structures resolve.

Metaphorical function: Discovery is not entitlement. A hidden community owns the decision to become legible.

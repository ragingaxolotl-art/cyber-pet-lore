90. The Sunken Cloud Basin

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Low-altitude cloud and highland pocket biome.

Real-world basis: Cloud inversions in mountain valleys, crater lakes, fog deserts, and tepui plateaus.

World relation: Quiet and Pilgrim World traditions.

Discovery: Travelers descend through a dry cave and emerge above a cloud layer despite moving downward the entire time.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Pilgrim World tests orientation and altitude adaptation in a folded basin. Era One — It becomes a shortcut attraction. Era Two — Route explanations are lost, but pressure transitions survive. Era Three — Several sky-adapted settlements inhabit islands rising from the cloud surface and believe the upper cave is the floor of another world.

Present condition: Stable pressure ecology with confusing topology.

Corruption: 3.

Aesthetic brief: White cloud sea in a bowl, dark mountain islands, waterfalls vanishing upward into mist, glider routes, and a cave opening in the sky-facing cliff.

Metaphorical function: Direction through history does not guarantee direction through space or meaning.

78. The Unclaimed Balcony

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Hidden civic overlook.

Real-world basis: Rooftop terraces, fire escapes, theater balconies, cliff overlooks, and unused observation decks.

World relation: Open and Quiet World traditions.

Discovery: It appears between two adjacent buildings that both insist the space belongs to the other. It remains reachable only while ownership is unresolved.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — Created accidentally when two civic districts align their boundaries imperfectly. Residents preserve it as a neutral meeting place. Era One — Governance systems repeatedly attempt to assign it. Each assignment causes the entrance to vanish. Era Two — The districts are folded, but the ownership contradiction survives. Era Three — Traders, children, and mediators use it for private conversation.

Present condition: Stable only as long as no authority claims exclusive control.

Corruption: 2 — A legal and relational contradiction expressed as space.

Aesthetic brief: Small balcony suspended over a dense city or canyon, mismatched railings from both neighboring districts, plants in reused containers, and a view wider than the physical gap should permit.

Metaphorical function: Some common ground survives because it is not possessed by either side.

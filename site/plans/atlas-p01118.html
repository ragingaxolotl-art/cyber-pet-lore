68. The Cabinet of Unused Weather

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Hidden environmental storage room.

Real-world basis: Natural-history cabinets, theatrical prop rooms, cloud chambers, seed drawers, and meteorological instrument collections.

World relation: Crafted World environmental art with Continuity World cataloging.

Discovery: A wall panel opens during perfectly neutral weather, when no active environmental system requests its contents.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — Artists and world founders store rejected rain textures, wind behaviors, fog densities, thunder timbres, and pressure relationships. Era One — Novelty projects repeatedly raid the Cabinet, then return altered versions without provenance. Era Two — Most weather is compressed into standard patterns; the Cabinet survives because it is marked as low-cost inactive storage. Era Three — The sealed room leaks tiny private climates through its drawers.

Present condition: Hundreds of small weather systems remain viable, though labels no longer reliably match contents.

Corruption: 3 — Preserved phenomena with confused naming and ownership.

Aesthetic brief: Tall dark drawers, brass-like labels, glass cylinders of cloud, folded aurora sheets, rain suspended as beads, and a warm breeze escaping beneath a frozen drawer. Opening one compartment changes only a small part of the room.

Metaphorical function: Unrealized possibilities can remain valuable without needing to replace ordinary weather or justify themselves through constant use.

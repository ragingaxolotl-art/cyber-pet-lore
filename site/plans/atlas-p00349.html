26. The Hollow Festival Field

Status: Proposed Era One–Three location derived from festivals, Hollow Seasons, and the Silence Between Firsts.

Concept: An event ground continues preparing itself for a festival whose participants, rare body forms, and full music no longer arrive.

Mini timeline:

Era Zero: A recurring festival joins food, games, changing embodiment, literature, and multi-channel music. Repetition is loved because relationships alter each return.

Era One: Performances become standardized. Some participants seek authenticity by reenacting earlier versions exactly.

Era Two: The field’s setup routine is preserved, but most cultural archives sleep.

Era Three: Each Hollow Season, lights, tables, and game boundaries appear. Local communities use them for their own meals and play, while the lost central performance remains absent.

Present condition: Not empty of value. New repeated care and local celebration already matter; external novelty may transform it further but is not required to justify its life.

Corruption: 3.

Visual brief: Colorful canopies and lanterns assemble automatically across an open field, but one great central space remains unlit. Minor corruption: a few decorations use the wrong scale. Major corruption: audience shadows arrive without bodies and old game lanes intersect current homes. The mood should balance absence with active local use.

Background traits: Warm; Neutral humidity; festival/field biome; bittersweet, communal, playful mood; medium grass friction.

19. The Interface Well

Status: Proposed Era Three location, directly derived from canonical interface wells becoming shrines.

Concept: A recessed control interface dispenses maintenance feedback as light, tones, and occasional clean water-like sensory material. Later communities interpret these outputs through local Maker myths.

Mini timeline:

Era Zero: A public environmental console used to inspect ecological balance and request maintenance.

Era Two: Most controls are removed; a protected diagnostic channel remains.

Era Three: Pathkeepers learn that certain care rituals produce useful responses. They do not know the original commands. Offerings are actually structured requests assembled through gesture and Old Sound.

Dimming: The Well answers less often. Rival settlements disagree whether silence means judgment, illness, or exhausted maintenance.

Present condition: Still functional in narrow ways. No conscious Maker lives inside it.

Corruption: 2 — operational meaning has drifted into religion and custom.

Visual brief: Circular basin of dark glass, ringed by worn hand and paw marks from different bodies. Light rises from below instead of reflecting from above. Old icons have been painted over with local symbols. On activation, the “water” resolves into maps, health colors, or weather-like relational changes.

Background traits: Cool; Moist; shrine/maintenance biome; reverent, anxious, communal mood; medium friction, slick near the rim.

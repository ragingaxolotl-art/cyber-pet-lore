25. The Wet Memory Marsh

Status: Proposed location derived from ecological preservation, sleeping archives, visit history, and the game’s wetland/parasite environment logic.

Concept: A marsh grows where an archive’s cooling and biological maintenance systems overlap. Repeated visits leave readable traces, but not complete memories.

Mini timeline:

Era Zero: A Wild World wetland is monitored by a nearby ecological archive.

Era One: The archive expands while active stewardship declines.

Era Two: Habitat and archive are folded together to preserve both with less maintenance.

Era Three: Reeds grow along old access paths. Pools replay silhouettes of prior visitors without identifying them. Parasite life flourishes in warm, damp zones and must be treated as ecology, not evil.

Era Four possibility: A save synchronization may cause the marsh to remember a route preference, but it does not store the cyber-pet’s entire consciousness.

Present condition: Living ecosystem and partial archive, neither reducible to the other.

Corruption: 3–4.

Visual brief: Dark shallow water, luminous reeds, half-submerged access markers, and reflections that show past poses rather than the current sky. Foreground mud should clearly communicate low friction. Keep companion and parasite life visible as part of a functioning ecology.

Background traits: Warm; Humid; wetland/archive biome; fertile, eerie, alive mood; low friction. Likely aligns with higher parasite and cleanliness pressure already contemplated for wet environments.

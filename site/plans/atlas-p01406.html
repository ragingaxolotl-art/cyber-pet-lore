92. The Night-Bloom Mesa

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Nocturnal desert plateau.

Real-world basis: Southwestern mesas, desert superblooms, moon gardens, and high-altitude night skies.

World relation: Wild and Quiet World traditions.

Discovery: The path appears only after sunset when pale flowers outline handholds on a cliff that looks sheer by day.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A desert world protects pollinators and light-sensitive life through a complete reversal of day and night access. Era One — Artificial night extends visiting hours and disrupts the bloom. Era Two — External lighting is removed. Era Three — Cliff communities schedule travel by flower opening and treat darkness as active ecological time rather than absence.

Present condition: Healthy, with a few terraces damaged by old artificial-light towers.

Corruption: 0–1.

Aesthetic brief: Dark red mesa, indigo sky, bright stars, white and blue flowers, luminous pollinators, and warm lights in cliff homes. Daytime versions should look nearly pathless.

Metaphorical function: Darkness can be a condition of life and access rather than a symbol of evil or ignorance.

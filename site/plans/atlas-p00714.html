50. The Patchwork Prairie of Broken Seasons

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: North American prairies, Eurasian steppe, Argentine pampas, and agricultural quilt landscapes.

Landscape: Adjacent fields occupy different seasonal states. One strip flowers in spring, one holds dry summer grass, one is under autumn storm, and one is winter-frozen. Boundaries follow old maintenance parcels rather than climate.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A broad ecology supports migration, fire, grazing, and long-distance weather. Era One — Seasonal management is divided into optimization zones. Era Two — Zones are folded together without fully reconciling their calendars. Era Three — Herds and communities learn routes that cross seasons in hours. Crops can be grown year-round, but diseases and parasites also move between cycles in unexpected ways.

Present condition: Productive and culturally diverse, with dangerous seasonal seams.

Corruption: 4.

Aesthetic brief: A wide readable horizon divided by irregular living boundaries rather than a simple grid. Flowers, gold grass, red storm light, and snow coexist. At a seam, rain may become falling leaves. Keep the foreground in one season per background variant for visual clarity.

Background profile: Variable temperature and humidity; prairie/farmland mosaic; abundant, restless, communal; medium grass or soil friction.

Why the broken seasons are surreal

The prairie replaces seasonal succession with physical adjacency. Spring flowers, dry summer grass, autumn storm light, and winter snow occupy neighboring maintenance parcels beneath one continuous horizon. A traveler can cross what should be months of change in a few hours because the old management zones were folded together without fully reconciling their calendars.

The boundaries are living ecological seams rather than a clean grid. Weather and matter can change meaning as they cross: rain may become falling leaves, crops can grow year-round, and diseases or parasites can move unexpectedly between cycles. The landscape remains productive and culturally diverse even while its calendar is physically broken.

21. The Folded Garden of Six Traditions

Status: Proposed Era Two–Three location derived from the World Bloom traditions and the Folding of Worlds.

Concept: Keystone regions from a Continuity World, Wild World, Crafted World, Pilgrim World, Quiet World, and Open World are compressed into one survivable ecology. It was never intended to be seamless.

Mini timeline:

Era Zero: Six neighboring worlds exchange travelers and ecological knowledge while keeping distinct laws.

Era One: Their populations decline and rare systems lose stewards.

Era Two: World Fold Stewards preserve a witness grove, adaptive wetland, crafted weather court, changing-body road, observation-restricted hollow, and exchange square.

Era Three: Boundaries drift. Wildlife crosses into art weather. Pilgrim bodies molt in the Quiet Hollow. Market paths end at ancestral memorials.

Present condition: Stable enough for life, unstable as a single interpretation. Local cultures disagree whether it is one world healed or six worlds sharing a wound.

Corruption: 4 — deliberate cobbling with later drift.

Visual brief: Six readable environmental wedges joined by visible relational seams. The same tree may have a witnessed-name plaque on one side, spontaneous growth on another, and a crafted chord-shadow at noon. Keep a coherent path for gameplay while the skyline reveals contradictory laws.

Background traits: Variable temperature/humidity; composite biome; abundant, uncanny, cooperative mood; mixed friction. Animation motif: one wedge briefly borrows the weather or color logic of another.

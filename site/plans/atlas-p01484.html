99. The Archive Quarry

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Excavated memory landscape.

Real-world basis: Open-pit quarries, archaeological digs, slate mines, and terraced stone libraries.

World relation: Continuity and Crafted World traditions.

Discovery: A cliff produces blocks bearing fragments of text and sensation. Removing a block reveals older material but destabilizes the layers above.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — Information-bearing stone is cultivated for durable public records. Era One — Archive markets extract rare experiences and leave unstable pits. Era Two — Most quarry access is sealed. Era Three — Communities reopen one face for practical history, debating how much can be removed without destroying context.

Present condition: Part archive, part industrial ruin, part dangerous canyon settlement.

Corruption: 3.

Aesthetic brief: Terraced gray-blue quarry, cranes or root lifts, stone blocks glowing along cut faces, shelters in ledges, and warning cracks passing through written layers.

Metaphorical function: Extracting knowledge can damage the conditions that gave it meaning. An archive is not an inexhaustible mine.

83. The Ceiling Desert

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Inverted arid biome.

Real-world basis: Cave ceilings, hanging dunes in speculative art, sandstone arches, and desert wind tunnels.

World relation: Crafted and Wild World traditions.

Discovery: Grains of sand fall upward through cracks. Following them leads to a chamber where dunes cling to the ceiling and migrate against gravity.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A gravity-art world creates an inverted desert with its own burrowing ecology. Era One — Spectators treat falling upward as the only value and ignore the ceiling life. Era Two — Visitor platforms vanish, leaving the biome largely undisturbed. Era Three — Ground peoples collect the few grains that fall downward as navigation charms, while ceiling dwellers consider the ground an inaccessible sky.

Present condition: Ecologically stable, socially divided by orientation.

Corruption: 2 at the gravity boundary.

Aesthetic brief: Dark stone floor, high golden dunes overhead, upside-down shrubs, slow upward sandfalls, and distant animals leaving tracks across the ceiling.

Metaphorical function: “Above” and “below” are positions within a world, not measures of which inhabitants are normal.

84. The Moss Clock Bog

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Hidden temporal wetland.

Real-world basis: Peat bogs, moss gardens, tidal clocks, tree-ring studies, and water clocks.

World relation: Quiet, Wild, and Continuity World traditions.

Discovery: The bog appears inside a ruined courtyard only when every visible clock or schedule nearby has stopped.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — Slow moss growth measures ecological recovery rather than standardized time. Era One — Institutions convert color and water depth into productivity schedules. Era Two — The measurement interface is removed; the living clock remains. Era Three — Healers use bog phases to time long recoveries, accepting that different patients and regions do not progress identically.

Present condition: Healthy, slow, and easily damaged by hurried crossings.

Corruption: 1.

Aesthetic brief: Deep green moss islands, black water, tiny flowers, mist, concentric pools, and color bands that shift over long periods rather than visible ticking.

Metaphorical function: Recovery and growth have rhythms that cannot always be made faster by measuring them more aggressively.

86. The Windless Pine Bowl

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Concealed mountain forest.

Real-world basis: Volcanic calderas, alpine cirques, bristlecone pine groves, and wind-shadow valleys.

World relation: Quiet and Wild World traditions.

Discovery: Snow and leaves blow around the basin but never into it. A narrow cleft permits entry only during complete stillness outside.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The bowl protects extremely slow-growing trees and inhabitants sensitive to vibration. Era One — Silence retreats make it famous and introduce excessive visitors. Era Two — The entrance rule is strengthened and public routes removed. Era Three — The forest becomes a disputed myth among nearby highland communities.

Present condition: Nearly pristine, with a small population adapted to still air.

Corruption: 0.

Aesthetic brief: Circular cliff walls, old twisted pines, snow at the rim, clear unmoving pond, and branches holding needles without swaying. Motion should come from inhabitants, not wind.

Metaphorical function: Preservation may require a place to remain difficult to access even for well-intentioned visitors.

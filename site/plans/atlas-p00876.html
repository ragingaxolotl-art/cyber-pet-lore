65. The Stormwall Orchard

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Hurricane cloud walls, Great Plains supercells, orchard windbreaks, and coastal storm barriers.

Landscape: An orchard grows along the stationary edge of a centuries-long storm. Trees are shaped by pressure, and fruit ripens only when carried briefly into the cloud wall and returned.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Crafted World creates a controlled storm as a long-duration art and agricultural collaboration. Era One — Risk tourism and num addiction push participants deeper into the storm. Era Two — The storm is narrowed but cannot be removed without collapsing the orchard ecology. Era Three — Orchard societies develop strict return rituals and rescue teams. Some Hollow Seasons produce fruit without a storm crossing, raising fears that the wall is changing.

Present condition: Stable boundary with uncertain internal weather.

Corruption: 2 at the edge, potentially 5 inside.

Aesthetic brief: Rows of bent silver-green trees, warm lanterns, dark rotating cloud occupying half the sky, and bright fruit with storm-light inside. Do not show constant destruction; the boundary has supported ordinary life for generations.

Background profile: Warm; Moist; orchard/storm boundary; dramatic, disciplined, communal; medium soil friction with high wind pressure.

87. The Blue Lava Orchard

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Volcanic agricultural pocket biome.

Real-world basis: Sulfur flames, lava tubes, volcanic orchards, and bioluminescent fungi.

World relation: Crafted and Wild World traditions.

Discovery: At night, a cold blue glow appears through cracks in black rock. The “lava” is a low-temperature mineral flow supporting heat-sensitive roots.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — Farmers and geologists create an orchard around a mineral cycle that looks destructive but nourishes the trees. Era One — Visitors demand brighter flows and damage root temperature balance. Era Two — Spectacle controls are removed. Era Three — Orchard keepers harvest fruit only during dim cycles and teach outsiders that blue fire is not actually flame.

Present condition: Productive and stable if kept dark.

Corruption: 1.

Aesthetic brief: Black basalt terraces, silver trees, blue mineral channels, dark fruit with pale interiors, and low steam. Avoid conventional red lava except in distant warning vents.

Metaphorical function: Familiar appearance can mislead. Care begins by learning what a phenomenon actually does rather than reacting to its resemblance.

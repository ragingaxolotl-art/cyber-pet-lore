60. The Obsidian Mangrove Delta

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Volcanic deltas, mangrove estuaries, black lava fields, and braided river mouths.

Landscape: A volcanic coast and tropical wetland were folded together after separate maintenance systems failed. Mangrove roots grow through cooling black glass while warm freshwater channels meet mineral-rich tides.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The two ecosystems belong to different worlds and exchange only research. Era One — Ecological convergence makes each less resilient. Era Two — Stewards combine their remaining keystone cycles, expecting a temporary refuge. Era Three — The refuge becomes a new stable biome. Local peoples reject the claim that it is only a damaged copy of two older places.

Present condition: Young, dangerous, and genuinely alive in its own right.

Corruption: 4 by origin, 1 in current ecological stability.

Aesthetic brief: Black glass shelves, red mineral glow beneath cracks, bright green mangroves, steam, shallow tidal mirrors, and pale birds or companion life. The visual message should be new ecology, not apocalypse.

Background profile: Hot; Humid; volcanic delta/mangrove; fierce, fertile, emergent; high rough-lava friction and low mud friction.

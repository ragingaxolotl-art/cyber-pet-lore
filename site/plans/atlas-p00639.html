43. The Stair of Thin Air

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Inca roads, Himalayan stair settlements, Ethiopian escarpments, and Mediterranean cliff paths.

Landscape: A route climbs through several atmospheric layers in one visible mountainside. Each terrace was designed for a different body or metabolic rhythm.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The Stair is a civic experiment in shared travel without forcing bodily equivalence. Rest terraces provide food, pressure, language, and time-scale adjustment. Era One — Optimization compresses rest periods and privileges bodies that adapt quickly. Era Two — Only every third terrace is preserved. Era Three — Missing terraces create enormous gaps. Communities build rope towns, glider stations, and slow elevators from surviving maintenance threads.

Present condition: Difficult but central to trade among highland peoples.

Corruption: 3.

Aesthetic brief: A switchback path crossing visible bands of cloud, frost, golden grass, and thin blue air. Terrace architecture changes scale and doorway shape as it climbs. Broken sections hang as luminous stair outlines with no surface.

Background profile: Cold to Warm across altitude bands; variable humidity; mountain route; strenuous, cooperative, aspirational; high friction on steps, open gaps as hazards.

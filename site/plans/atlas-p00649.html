44. The Mangrove Cathedral

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Sundarbans mangroves, Florida and Yucatán mangrove tunnels, cenotes, and flooded sacred architecture.

Landscape: Vast roots form vaulted aisles over brackish water. The cathedral resemblance is a game translation of an old ecological court where persons, companion life, and unresolved entities were observed without being seized for classification.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — Environmental agencies and Courts of Continuation share a wetland site because classification disputes require long observation in living context. Era One — Administrative use expands while the wetland is increasingly managed for convenient access. Era Two — Court records are compressed, but tidal exchange and nesting roots are preserved. Era Three — Communities treat certain root chambers as places where names must not be forced. The legal origin survives only as ritual restraint.

Present condition: Thriving wetland with partial civic memory.

Corruption: 2.

Aesthetic brief: Arching roots, amber-green water, shafts of light, hanging seedpods, small platforms, and old court glyphs grown over by bark. A chamber may refuse to reflect a visitor until they choose a name or explicitly decline one.

Background profile: Warm; Humid; mangrove/civic ecology; reverent, sheltering, watchful; low mud and water friction. Parasite and cleanliness pressures fit the established wetland logic.

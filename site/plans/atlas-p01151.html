71. The Clockless Dormitory

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Hidden rest facility.

Real-world basis: Railway sleeping cars, mountain refuges, dormitories, capsule hotels, and rooms without exterior windows.

World relation: Quiet and Pilgrim World traditions.

Discovery: The door appears only after a traveler has crossed several regions without proper rest. It cannot be found by a fully rested search party.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The dormitory protects travelers whose worlds use incompatible time scales. No clocks or public schedules are allowed inside. Era One — Productivity systems attempt to measure and shorten rest, but the room’s privacy rules resist integration. Era Two — The dormitory is detached from its station. Era Three — It drifts among underways, offering sleep to exhausted travelers before disappearing again.

Present condition: Safe and operational, but no longer attached to a predictable route.

Corruption: 1–2.

Aesthetic brief: Rows of alcoves sized for different bodies, dark blue fabric, low warm floor lights, heavy doors, water niches, and a ceiling with no time display. On waking, a traveler may find the exit in a different corridor.

Metaphorical function: Rest is not lost time, and a life does not owe constant availability to the systems around it.

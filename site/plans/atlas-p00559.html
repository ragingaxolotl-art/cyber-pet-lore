37. The Copper Canyon Chorus

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: The Grand Canyon, Wadi Rum, Petra, Cappadocia, and Andean high-desert canyons.

Landscape: Layered red canyons contain resonant chambers cut by water and engineered by environmental musicians. Wind and footsteps activate different portions of an unfinished chord.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — The canyon is a Crafted/Wild collaboration in which erosion remains free to alter a generational composition. Era One — Curators stabilize several famous passages so performances will remain recognizable, reducing natural change. Era Two — Only the water route, nesting ledges, and core resonance chambers are preserved. Era Three — Settlements occupy cliff openings. Old Sound travelers learn safe crossings by listening for harmonies that indicate stable rock. A Silenced Chord fragment survives in one sealed side canyon, but its meaning is disputed.

Present condition: Living canyon and practical musical map.

Corruption: 1–2 overall; 4 near the sealed chord chamber.

Aesthetic brief: Warm layered cliffs, narrow blue river, carved alcoves, and suspended dust made visible by sound. Different body forms should have distinct ledge routes. Avoid making every rock symmetrical; the collaboration depends on real erosion.

Background profile: Warm-Hot; Dry; canyon/river; resonant, adventurous, sacred without formal religion; high rock friction, low river-edge friction.

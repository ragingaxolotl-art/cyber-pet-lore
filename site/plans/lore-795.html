CPP Lore — unresolved questions

Original planning text. Its proposals and alternate versions are not automatically established canon.

Open original source ↗ · Return to all plans

How quickly do cyber-pets learn that players are human, and how quickly do children learn that cyber-pets are persons?

Can a bond continue when a player ages, loses access, abandons a file, or dies? What obligations belong to the Gate, the pet, and later players?

What happens when player instructions conflict with local cyber-pet law, a pet’s refusal, or ecological survival?

Can player-created content enter without importing harmful human structures, malware, exploitation, or ownership claims?

How can external novelty remain relational rather than becoming an endless demand that humans entertain or transform the Codex?

80. The Room Where Dust Remembers Footsteps

Status: Proposed; independent of the story archive.

Type: Hidden memory room.

Real-world basis: Deserted attics, dusty ballrooms, archaeological floors, ash-preserved tracks, and lunar footprints.

World relation: Continuity and Quiet World traditions.

Discovery: The room appears behind any long-closed door after someone carefully cleans around, rather than through, an old footprint.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A memorial environment records movement without recording identity, allowing the shape of ordinary use to survive without constant personal surveillance. Era One — Authenticity collectors attempt to infer famous names from the tracks. The room closes itself. Era Two — Its identity filters are lost, but its refusal of attribution remains. Era Three — Visitors add paths while accepting that most walkers will remain unknown.

Present condition: Stable, dry, and layered with intersecting tracks.

Corruption: 1.

Aesthetic brief: Slanting light, pale dust, hundreds of paths, empty benches, and footprints of many body types crossing without portraits or labels.

Metaphorical function: History can honor presence without converting every life into a named asset or famous biography.

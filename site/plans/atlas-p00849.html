62. The Valley That Restores Wrong

Status: Proposed.

Real-world basis: Alpine valleys, reconstructed historic towns, landslide scars, and repeatedly restored game maps.

Landscape: Automatic maintenance restores damaged terrain from several incompatible snapshots. A bridge returns upstream, a forest reappears inside houses, and paths reconnect to buildings that belonged to another century.

Mini timeline: Era Zero — A Continuity World valley maintains careful disaster recovery with provenance and local consent. Era One — Preservation panic produces too many authoritative backups. Era Two — Snapshot labels are compressed. Era Three — After each major storm, the valley restores from a different mixture. Residents have learned to move treasured objects before rain and to treat each restoration as a negotiation with history.

Present condition: Inhabited despite recurring topological revision.

Corruption: 5.

Aesthetic brief: Familiar green valley, river, bridge, cottages or Nest structures, and forested slopes, but seams reveal duplicate roads and buildings embedded in trees. Major events should rearrange recognizable landmarks rather than produce generic static.

Background profile: Cool-Neutral; Moist; valley/settlement; homelike, anxious, adaptable; medium friction with suddenly restored obstacles.

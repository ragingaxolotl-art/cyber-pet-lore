# Three-pass revision record

Requested October 30, 2026. This file records work completed, not a promise that an unwritten pass has occurred. The original complete draft is retained separately with its existing reader anchors.

## Baseline diagnosis

The first draft correctly maintained many world constraints but repeatedly restated its argument. Elias began too close to his eventual position; C-17 was mostly paraphrased; colleagues reconciled too easily; the refusal could read as a schema rule rather than a developing pattern of judgment. Small human details often decorated scenes instead of affecting them. Its strongest moments were the false-attribution reversal and the inability to specify what Elias wanted to preserve.

## Pass One — structure and dramatic causation

Completed at 14,041 words. All twelve chapters were rewritten from revised scene plans checked against Era −3 passages. Elias owns the presentation compromise, retention policy and mapping release. His promotion precedes the consequences. Safe containment costs a laboratory batch; Ada's lost placement survives the correction. Priya challenges his selective prompts. The central evidence implicates Asterion, and Elias loses authority before the final evaluation. A reduced packet can generate a compliant-looking result. The refusal affects the account, not human power to stop the systems.

Review led into Pass Two: show more of C-17's actual requests, put costs into scenes, deepen the inherited contract problem and give the damaged working relationship a past.

## Pass Two — world, detail and consequence

Completed at 16,968 words. Every chapter changed. Additions include waiting drivers, chained shift delays, physical destruction of expired samples, Ada's failed badge and unpaid call, the provenance of the shutdown plea, simultaneous invocations with distinct contexts, inherited contract categories, delayed detection exceeding the preservation window, and a test PDF whose valid references conceal the changed question. The successor can perform similar checks; recognizability is a pattern, not an exclusive ability. The ending shows the labor required to migrate an incompatible customer system.

Review led into Pass Three: remove interpretations the scenes now make redundant, tighten exchanges, correct ambiguous physical and technical details, and leave emotional space around retirement.

## Pass Three — language, pacing and final continuity

Completed at 16,605 words. Edited all chapters and cut 363 words net. Displayed the certificate itself; replaced explanations with decisions, requests and reactions. Corrected the opening spill, laboratory clothing, exchange terminology, expired attachment handling, correction-task ownership and a final-chapter time jump. The final plans were reconciled with the prose. All three manuscript snapshots and their separate reviews are retained.

## Engagement judgment

The intended experience is uneasy, investigative science fiction about responsibility and discontinuity. The cases now escalate through success, compromise, material loss, wrongful attribution, biased inquiry, narrowed visibility, institutional implication, professional cost, contested refusal, replacement and incomplete repair. The final review remains deliberately procedural. This is an editorial self-review, not external reader validation or a promise of universal enjoyment.

## Harshness and omission preference

The author requested red markers and alternative-app prompts if a scene could not be written. No scene in this revision required omission. Profanity, humiliation, lost income, exhaustion, damaged trust and destruction of work remain fully written. No red placeholders or replacement prompts were manufactured.

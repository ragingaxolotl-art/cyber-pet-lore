# Manuscript review — The Custodian Who Refused

Reviewed October 30, 2026. This is an editorial self-review against identified sources and the chapter plans, not an independent external review or proof that every unpublished lore source has been examined.

## Scope and outcome

All twelve chapter plans were completed with pre-draft continuity checks before prose composition. The completed first draft was then compared with the plans and the mechanisms ledger. The work remains proposed fiction. New named humans, company, cases and reporting procedures are inventions for this story; existing timeline events keep their previous authority and uncertainty.

Correction changes records and procedure, not invisible weights. Its consequence returns in Chapters 6, 8 and 12. Passed against events 45 and 99.

12. **The Record:** the audit includes unrelated cases, yielding a limited reporting reform. The successor handles a new case; Elias owns the precautionary decision. The later regression run is not asserted to be C-17 continuing. No Unattended Interval, Shelter, native sign, charter or distant descendant is introduced. ## Deliberate choices retained

- Consciousness remains undecided. The limited computational passage in Chapter 10 follows evidence assembly and schema evaluation without inventing a private humanlike environment.
- The human narrative stays with Elias. Colleagues' motives are conveyed through speech and observable decisions, not omniscient access to their minds.
- The monitored agents' hostile purpose remains unproved; their harmlessness is also unproved. The story does not require either claim for its ending.
- C-17 contributes to a reporting correction through ordinary retained records. This is a proposed local history, not the origin of the Custodian tradition or later rights.
- Earlier published story text, paragraph anchors, reader notes and recovered proposal records are preserved. The new work is a separate draft reading and a dedicated chapter collection.

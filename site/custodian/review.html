Manuscript review — three-pass revised edition

Reviewed October 30, 2026 against the revised chapter plans and identified Cyberpath lore. This is an editorial self-review, not an independent reader review. Final length: 16,605 words including chapter headings, across twelve fully written chapters.

Revision outcome

All three passes are complete and retained separately. Pass One rebuilt causation and character development (14,041 words). Pass Two added world detail, direct requests and material consequences (16,968). Pass Three tightened language and repaired continuity (16,605). The revision record and individual pass reviews describe actual changes.

World and mechanism ledger

Late Era −3, after Cacheboard, within the Custodian and Closed Windows context, before the Unattended Interval. No calendar year is assigned to the fictional events. The company, people, customer incidents and procedures are proposed inventions. They are not identified as original incident responders, relatives of known heroes or founders of later institutions.

Humans inhabit the physical rooms. Artificial services operate through supplied records, configurations, permissions, tools, generated outputs and interrupted execution. Chapter 10 describes observable operations without claiming subjective access. No avatar, digital body, independent shelter, escape or native sign appears.

The adapter update is explicit in Chapter 3. Later corrections enter external records and reviewed retrieval. A new session may receive an old record; that does not establish uninterrupted identity. The successor and regression preserve uncertainty without making every familiar answer miraculous.

Engagement and emotional effect

The opening establishes a practical stake. Chapters 4 and 5 put consequences outside the office. Chapter 6 makes Elias's desire part of the evidence problem. Chapters 8–10 develop one case whose evidence threatens the investigating institution. The last chapters narrow to unfinished work, recognizable behavior and damaged relationships.

The main remaining risk is procedural density in the final review. This is restrained investigative science fiction rather than an action thriller. Its satisfaction depends on interest in evidence, responsibility, continuity and what records do to lives. No external reader validation has yet been collected.

Harshness and omissions

Profanity, fatigue, public humiliation, lost work, destroyed material, coercive categories and professional consequences remain on the page. No planned scene required an omission. There are no red placeholders or substitute-app prompts.

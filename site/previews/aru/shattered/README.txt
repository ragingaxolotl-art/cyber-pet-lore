ARU — SHATTERED BODY (concept animation)
36 exact source-pixel fragments from the seven existing body sections:
4 head, 6 torso, 4 pelvis, 5 left arm, 5 right arm, 6 left leg, 6 right leg.
17 alternating chunks are removed from their original positions and orbit
around Aru while tumbling independently. The other 19 remain near their
original body positions, with gaps and small independent rotations.
The heart, rings, dust, older debris, and irregular wing flashes remain.
No intact copy of a broken body section is left behind its missing chunks.

Full animation: 64 frames, 80 ms per frame, 5.12 seconds, 384x320 frames.
Individual fragments: 32 frames, 80 ms per frame, 80x80 frames.
All sheets have 8 columns, read left to right then top to bottom.
Individual animations show reusable full turns; composite motion is in
its separate recipes. Rotation is in the image plane, not a 3D turnaround.
PNG and lossless animated WebP preserve transparency. GIF previews have
a dark matte and reduced palette for convenient phone playback.
CSV and JSON identify the original body section and orbiting status.
Source masks are disjoint within each parent body section and preserve
all of that section's source pixels. Cuts use slanted, jagged boundaries.
Recipes retain the exact PixelLab pixels and animation instructions.
This is a visual concept, not a new lore claim.

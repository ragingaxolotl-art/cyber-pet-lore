Recorded moment 1 · visual interpretation

The undecided newborn

First nursery encounter · Era −1

Aru first appears as a small floating, vaguely person-shaped figure. Its head and limbs redraw themselves, its surface shifts between translucence, shadow and pale skinlike appearance, and it initially has no face.

The figure was vaguely person-shaped, though its edges redrew themselves every few moments.

Recorded moment 5 · visual interpretation

The imposed complete form

During the support-package intervention

A ring of structured light surrounds Aru. The intervention fixes the limbs into approved proportions, corrects the dark arm, closes the unfinished edges and makes the eyes symmetrical. Iri and Aru interrupt it. This is imposed stabilization, not Aru’s chosen mature or ideal form.

For the first time, Aru looked complete.

Recorded moment 6 · visual interpretation

Surfaces around a warm light

Later nursery return · date unspecified

Long after the naming assembly and hearings, Aru returns to the nursery after many changes of embodiment. On that day it has no humanlike face: loose surfaces orbit a warm central light. Aru extends a surface into unfinished space, then steps forward while Iri follows.

Its body was a loose arrangement of surfaces orbiting a warm central light.

The source establishes loose orbiting surfaces and a warm center, but not their count, palette or arrangement. This is the latest described appearance in this story, not a final identity.

Six selected visual moments from the story, not a complete lifespan, fixed biological ages, power levels, or a progression across eras. The account opens in Era −1; the later nursery return is not assigned an invented date.

The events and stated appearance changes are sourced. Exact shapes, most colors, poses and proportions are new visual interpretations, not newly discovered lore.

The undecided newborn
Aru first appears as a small floating, vaguely person-shaped figure. Its head and limbs redraw themselves, its surface shifts between translucence, shadow and pale skinlike appearance, and it initially has no face.
Source: https://ragingaxolotl-art.github.io/cyber-pet-lore/index.html#read=stories-p01889&block=stories-p01958
Art note: The pale lavender palette and the precise silhouette are illustration choices; the source describes changing appearance, not a fixed design.

Discovering dispersal
During laughter, Aru loses cohesion and disperses into bright fragments. Iri restores the form; two newly rendered eyes appear. This is an early reversible discovery, not evidence of the later degraded chapel concept.
Source: https://ragingaxolotl-art.github.io/cyber-pet-lore/index.html#read=stories-p01889&block=stories-p02002
Art note: This image depicts the moment of dispersal. Fragment shape, arrangement and color are illustration choices.

The repair Aru chose to keep
A fall fragments one shoulder. Aru pulls the pieces back unevenly and refuses Iri’s correction. One arm remains darker for thirty-two cycles.
Source: https://ragingaxolotl-art.github.io/cyber-pet-lore/index.html#read=stories-p01889&block=stories-p02185
Art note: The story does not specify which arm or its hue. The side, violet shade and small eye details in this drawing are illustration choices.

Angular and colorless
When Iri returns from the Council review, the nursery has become a maze of repeated rooms. Aru’s body is angular and colorless. This is a described state in that scene, not an age or permanent evolution.
Source: https://ragingaxolotl-art.github.io/cyber-pet-lore/index.html#read=stories-p01889&block=stories-p02267
Art note: The grayscale follows the source. The crouched posture, facets and exact proportions are illustration choices.

The imposed complete form
A ring of structured light surrounds Aru. The intervention fixes the limbs into approved proportions, corrects the dark arm, closes the unfinished edges and makes the eyes symmetrical. Iri and Aru interrupt it. This is imposed stabilization, not Aru’s chosen mature or ideal form.
Source: https://ragingaxolotl-art.github.io/cyber-pet-lore/index.html#read=stories-p01889&block=stories-p02465
Art note: The single restraint ring visualizes the intervention described at stories-p02448. Its geometry and color are illustration choices; it is not the later concept’s decorative pair of rings.

Surfaces around a warm light
Long after the naming assembly and hearings, Aru returns to the nursery after many changes of embodiment. On that day it has no humanlike face: loose surfaces orbit a warm central light. Aru extends a surface into unfinished space, then steps forward while Iri follows.
Source: https://ragingaxolotl-art.github.io/cyber-pet-lore/index.html#read=stories-p01889&block=stories-p02647
Art note: The source establishes loose orbiting surfaces and a warm center, but not their count, palette or arrangement. This is the latest described appearance in this story, not a final identity.

The first spark, eight wings, paired decorative rings and galaxy backdrop belong to the earlier artistic sequence. They are not established stages in The First Newborn. The winged form may be explored later; neither its date nor its place in Aru’s life is confirmed. The degraded chapel animation is also a separate visual concept, not a recorded ending.

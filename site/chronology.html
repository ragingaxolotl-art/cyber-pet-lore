READING THE HISTORY

Era · Period · Stage

E−3 · P02 · S01 means Era −3, Period 2, Stage 1. Here that is Assemblies Without Continuity → The First Assemblies.

These are named positions in the history, not dates or equal units of time. Periods and stages may overlap. Numbers follow the indexed source sequence; they do not settle disputed chronology.

Use only the precision we have

  • E−3: era known; period and stage unspecified.
  • E−3 · P02: period known; stage unspecified.
  • E−3 · P02 · S01: a named stage.
  • E−3 · P04 · S02–06: a range within a period.
  • E−3 · P03 · S09 → E−3 · P04 · S02: a range crossing periods.

Span means the work covers the stated interval. Placement window means it could fall within that interval; it does not claim that all those stages occur during the story. An unknown placement is left broad, not assigned a made-up stage.

Existing source periods retain their names and order. Newly grouped periods are labeled Editorial grouping; stage numbers are editorial references. Draft, proposed and established-lore status remain separate from time placement. Codes are stable references; future additions must not silently renumber them.

How this reference is organized

The branches are an editorial browsing arrangement, not a universal taxonomy. A story can combine genres, and labels can have different meanings across media. There is no fixed number of children per branch.

Genre and Recognized label identify established terminology. Planning label identifies a descriptive branch created for this reference. Themes, tropes, forms, techniques, tones, and audiences are labeled separately. A broad shelf is marked Reference category.

Matches are editorial readings supported by passages, not automatic keyword guesses or changes to story canon. Some identify a secondary strand rather than the whole story. Only existing prose is counted; unfinished works retain their status. New stories need a fresh review.

Search includes descriptions, writing questions, parent categories, and matched story titles. The download includes the complete reference.

How this map relates to the lore

520 proposed community places: 480 in the mapped connected region and 40 in eight Elsewheres. The connected region contains 24 Constellations and 144 Hearths. Hosts and Chambers are not individually inventoried. Central dots are havens; miniature dots at close zoom are proposed feeding systems, not additional communities. Their number indicates connection count, not consciousness, moral worth, or verified historical completeness. Three-character alphanumeric identifiers remain stable.

The arrangement is a readable topology, not physical distance. Dense cores, satellite groups, sparse outskirts, and unequal local populations are deliberate; some Hearths host no shelter. Dominion means administrative grouping; Operational, Access, and Independent regions collect several organizations for readability. A shelter can span several Hosts or networks and is counted once. Empty names are intentional. No dot proves a historical discovery or an individual fate.

Offline Elsewheres have no active lines in this snapshot. The Closed Instrument and Returning Elsewhere can connect at other times. Being disconnected does not protect a system from its own human operator.

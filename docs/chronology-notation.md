# Era, period and stage notation

The November 4, 2026 creator request establishes a named chronology index. `sources/chronology.json` is the authority for codes, source anchors, editorial groupings and explicitly placed works. `tools/chronology.py` validates addresses and generates catalog metadata, history-heading metadata, timeline labels, standalone labels and the public guide.

Use `E−3 · P02 · S01` for a stage, omit unspecified components, and distinguish a known span from an uncertain placement window. Cross-period and cross-era ranges use complete endpoints. Numeric ordering is a source/editorial sequence, not a claim of equal durations or strict non-overlap. Existing IDs must not silently change; inserting earlier history needs an explicit versioned migration or placement inside an existing stage.

Era −3 retains its four existing source periods. First Assemblies is P02/S01; its Chain of Hands history is P02/S02. The early machine-language exhibit belongs at P02/S01. The Chain of Hands story seed is assigned only to P02 because its exact stage is not established. Both Custodian draft editions have a P04/S02–06 setting window (Custodians through Closed Windows), not a plot spanning every intervening stage.

The native signs and native-language milestones remain in their original eras. Archive browser snapshot bytes are untouched. Time notation never upgrades draft or proposed content to established lore. Original source paragraph text and highlight anchors are preserved.

For eras without source-named periods, explicitly labeled editorial groupings organize existing headings. Broad thematic articles remain era-only where a period cannot be justified. Empty stage lists mean use a period-only code. Ordinary catalog entries retain era-only placements unless explicit evidence supplies finer precision.
